DC-DC Buck, Boost & Buck-Boost Converters Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Switched-mode DC-DC converters achieve high efficiency ($> 90\%$) compared to linear dissipative regulators. Buck converters step down average voltage ($V_{out} = D V_{in}$), Boost converters step up ($V_{out} = \frac{V_{in}}{1-D}$), and Buck-Boost topologies provide inverting voltage step-up/step-down ($V_{out} = \frac{D}{1-D} V_{in}$).
