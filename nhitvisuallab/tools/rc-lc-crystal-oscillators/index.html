Sinusoidal Oscillators Lab (RC, LC & Crystal) — Theory & Governing Equations

Sinusoidal oscillators convert DC electrical power into continuous periodic AC waveforms without external input. According to the Barkhausen Criterion, sustained oscillations require $|A\beta| = 1$ and a total loop phase shift of $0^\circ$ or $360^\circ$. RC networks operate at audio frequencies, LC tanks at radio frequencies, and quartz crystals provide frequency references with stability $> 10^{-6}$.
