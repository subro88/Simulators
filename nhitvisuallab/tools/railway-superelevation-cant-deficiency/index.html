Railway Superelevation & Cant Deficiency Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Railway superelevation (Cant $C = \frac{G V^2}{127 R}$) raises the outer rail above the inner rail on curves to counteract centrifugal force. Because trains travel at varying speeds, cant deficiency ($C_d \le 76\text{ mm}$ for BG) is permitted for high-speed trains, preventing excess wear and passenger discomfort.

📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

Railway superelevation (Cant $C = \frac{G V^2}{127 R}$) raises the outer rail above the inner rail on curves to counteract centrifugal force. Because trains travel at varying speeds, cant deficiency ($C_d \le 76\text{ mm}$ for BG) is permitted for high-speed trains, preventing excess wear and passenger discomfort.

Adjust the sliders and input fields on the left. Click Calculate & Analyze to run the solvers.

3. Building the Diagrams & Diagnostic Telemetry

The interactive canvas dynamically visualizes stress blocks, shear failure planes, track banking profiles, runway lengths, or pile skin resistance based on live analytical equations.
