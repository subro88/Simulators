Structural Retrofitting & FRP Jacketing Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Fiber Reinforced Polymer (FRP) wrapping is an advanced composite retrofitting technique. Continuous CFRP/GFRP jackets exert passive lateral confining pressure ($f_l = \frac{2 t_f f_{fu}}{D}$) on concrete under axial dilation, vastly boosting compressive strength ($f_{cc}' = f_{co}' + 3.3 f_l$) and ultimate failure strain.

📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

Fiber Reinforced Polymer (FRP) wrapping is an advanced composite retrofitting technique. Continuous CFRP/GFRP jackets exert passive lateral confining pressure ($f_l = \frac{2 t_f f_{fu}}{D}$) on concrete under axial dilation, vastly boosting compressive strength ($f_{cc}' = f_{co}' + 3.3 f_l$) and ultimate failure strain.
