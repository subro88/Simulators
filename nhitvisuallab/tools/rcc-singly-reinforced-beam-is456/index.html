RCC Singly Reinforced Beam Design Lab (IS 456) — Theory & Governing Equations

In IS 456:2000 Limit State of Collapse (Flexure), the rectangular-parabolic compressive stress block develops a total concrete compression force ($C_u = 0.36 f_{ck} b x_u$) acting at $0.42 x_u$ from the extreme compression fiber. For under-reinforced sections ($x_u \le x_{u,max}$), tensile steel yields first ($f_{st} = 0.87 f_y$), ensuring warning cracks prior to ultimate failure.
