RLC Transient Response & Damping Lab — Detailed Theory & Mathematics

Transient phenomena in RLC networks govern system stability, pulse rise times, and overshoot under switching excitations. Second-order differential equations yield three damping regimes based on the damping factor $\zeta = \frac{R}{2}\sqrt{\frac{C}{L}}$: underdamped ($\zeta < 1$), critically damped ($\zeta = 1$), and overdamped ($\zeta > 1$).
