Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE ME/S3/MED Shaft Couplings & Universal Joints Lab

1. Flange Coupling Design Equations

Shaft diameter $d = \left(\frac{16 T}{\pi \tau}\right)^{1/3}$. Flange hub diameter $D = 2d$, Pitch circle diameter $D_1 = 3d$, Outer flange diameter $D_2 = 4d$.

2. Bolt Shear & Crushing Stress

Torque capacity $T = n \cdot \frac{\pi}{4} d_b^2 \cdot \tau_b \cdot \frac{D_1}{2}$. Crushing strength $T = n \cdot (d_b \cdot t_f) \cdot \sigma_{cb} \cdot \frac{D_1}{2}$.

3. Oldham's Coupling Kinematics

Inversion of double slider crank chain used for connecting two shafts having lateral axial parallel offset.

4. Hooke's Universal Joint

Velocity ratio $\frac{\omega_2}{\omega_1} = \frac{\cos \theta}{1 - \sin^2 \theta \sin^2 \alpha_1}$. Two joints in series (Double Cardan) cancel out speed fluctuations.
