📖 Mechanical Principles & Mathematical Derivations Syllabus Reference ES102/ES104

1. Apparatus Geometry & Differential Kinematics

A Differential Axle and Wheel (Weston's Differential Pulley principle) comprises three coaxial cylinders rigidly keyed together to a common horizontal shaft: an effort wheel of diameter \(D\) (radius \(R\)), a larger drum/axle of diameter \(d_1\) (radius \(r_1\)), and a smaller drum/axle of diameter \(d_2\) (radius \(r_2\)), where \(d_1 > d_2\). A continuous flexible cord loop wraps around both axles in opposite directions, supporting a floating snatch pulley with load hook \(W\).

$$\text{Linear distance moved by effort in 1 rev } (y) = \pi D = 2 \pi R$$ $$\text{Cord wound onto larger axle in 1 rev } (\Delta L_1) = \pi d_1 = 2 \pi r_1$$ $$\text{Cord unwound from smaller axle in 1 rev } (\Delta L_2) = \pi d_2 = 2 \pi r_2$$ $$\text{Net shortening of load supporting loop } (\Delta L_{\text{net}}) = 2 \pi (r_1 - r_2) = \pi (d_1 - d_2)$$ $$\text{Linear lift of load hook } (x) = \frac{\Delta L_{\text{net}}}{2} = \pi \left(\frac{d_1 - d_2}{2}\right) = \pi (r_1 - r_2)$$ $$VR = \frac{y}{x} = \frac{2 \pi R}{\pi (r_1 - r_2)} = \mathbf{\frac{2 R}{r_1 - r_2} = \frac{2 D}{d_1 - d_2}}$$

2. Torque Balance & Self-Locking Boundary (\(\eta \le 50\%\))

The suspended load exerts a net unwinding (back-drive) torque on the common spindle:

$$\tau_{\text{back}} = \left(\frac{W}{2}\right) r_1 - \left(\frac{W}{2}\right) r_2 = \frac{W}{2} (r_1 - r_2)$$ $$\tau_{\text{friction}} = \mu_{\text{bearing}} (W + P) r_{\text{journal}} + \tau_0$$

Irreversible Self-Locking Criterion: The machine is strictly self-locking (will not back-drive or drop the suspended load upon sudden effort release) if and only if:

$$\tau_{\text{back}} \le \tau_{\text{friction}} \iff \mathbf{\eta \le 50\%}$$

When \(d_1 - d_2\) is small (\(\approx 2 \text{ to } 3\text{ cm}\)), \(\tau_{\text{back}}\) is minimal and readily arrested by journal stiction (\(\eta \approx 35\% - 44\% < 50\%\)). When \(d_1 - d_2\) is large (\(\ge 10\text{ cm}\)), back-drive torque exceeds friction (\(\eta > 50\%\)), creating an overhauling runaway hazard.

3. Universal Law of the Machine & Frictional Limits

$$P = m \cdot W + C$$ $$MA_{\text{max}} = \frac{1}{m}, \qquad \eta_{\text{max}} = \frac{1}{m \cdot VR} \times 100\%$$ $$P_f = P - \frac{W}{VR}, \qquad F_f = (P \cdot VR) - W$$

📚 Comprehensive User Guide & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. Apparatus Overview & Mechanical Principle
A Differential Axle and Wheel is a powerful mechanical hoist utilizing two stepped cylindrical drums of slightly different diameters (\(d_1 > d_2\)) keyed rigidly to a common horizontal shaft together with a large effort wheel of diameter \(D\). When the shaft rotates, one drum winds up cable while the other unwinds cable, achieving large mechanical advantage and velocity ratio \(VR = \frac{2 D}{d_1 - d_2}\) with compact dimensions.
2. Experimental Protocol & Multi-Load Sweeps
  1. Measure effort wheel diameter \(D\) and stepped axle diameters \(d_1, d_2\) using vernier calipers.
  2. Calculate kinematic Velocity Ratio \(VR = \frac{2 D}{d_1 - d_2} = \frac{2 R}{r_1 - r_2}\).
  3. Suspend a calibrated weight \(W\) (e.g. \(10\text{ kgf}\)) from the snatch pulley hook.
  4. Incrementally add fractional weights to the effort pan until the wheel initiates steady, uniform rotation (or click Auto-Balance Effort).
  5. Record \(W\) and \(P\), and click Log Current Reading.
  6. Repeat across loads up to \(40\text{ kgf}\), or click Auto Sweep Standard Range.
  7. Review the regression card for empirical Law of the Machine constants (\(m\) and \(C\)).
3. Self-Locking vs. Overhauling Verification
Click Release Effort Pan (Self-Locking Test) to simulate sudden loss of input effort:
  • In Standard Model (\(\Delta d = 3\text{ cm}, \mu=0.14\)): \(\eta \approx 40\% - 44\% < 50\%\). The rig holds firmly in static equilibrium without dropping.
  • In Overhauling Demo Model (\(\Delta d = 10\text{ cm}, \mu=0.08\)): \(\eta > 50\%\). Net back-drive torque exceeds bearing friction, causing the load to drop rapidly in an overhauling runaway with audio alarm.
