RCC Short Column & Helical Ties Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Short compression columns resist vertical axial structural loads with minimum accidental eccentricity ($e_{min} = \frac{L}{500} + \frac{D}{30} \le 20\text{ mm}$). Per IS 456 Clause 39.3, ultimate axial capacity is $P_u = 0.4 f_{ck} A_c + 0.67 f_y A_{sc}$. Helical spiral reinforcement provides triaxial confinement, increasing strength by $5\%$ ($1.05 P_u$).

📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

Short compression columns resist vertical axial structural loads with minimum accidental eccentricity ($e_{min} = \frac{L}{500} + \frac{D}{30} \le 20\text{ mm}$). Per IS 456 Clause 39.3, ultimate axial capacity is $P_u = 0.4 f_{ck} A_c + 0.67 f_y A_{sc}$. Helical spiral reinforcement provides triaxial confinement, increasing strength by $5\%$ ($1.05 P_u$).
