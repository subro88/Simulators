📖 Electrostatic Engine & Governing Formulations

A parallel plate capacitor is an electrostatic charge-storage device consisting of two conducting plates separated by a dielectric medium. The electrostatic capacitance is purely determined by the geometry of the plates and the permittivity of the intervening insulator.

1. Capacitance & Vacuum Permittivity ($\varepsilon_0$):
For plates of overlap area $A$ separated by gap $d$: $$C = \frac{\kappa \varepsilon_0 A}{d}$$ where $\varepsilon_0 = 8.85419 \times 10^{-12}\text{ F/m}$ and $\kappa$ is the relative permittivity ($\kappa = 1.0$ for vacuum/air).
2. Thermodynamic Regimes: Battery Connected vs. Isolated:
• Battery Connected ($V = V_0 = \text{Const}$): $$Q = C V_0 \propto \frac{1}{d}, \quad U = \frac{1}{2} C V_0^2 \propto \frac{1}{d}, \quad E = \frac{V_0}{d}$$ • Battery Isolated ($Q = Q_0 = \text{Const}$): $$V = \frac{Q_0}{C} \propto d, \quad U = \frac{Q_0^2}{2C} \propto d, \quad E = \frac{Q_0}{\varepsilon_0 A} = \text{Constant}$$ *(Pulling plates apart in the isolated state increases stored electrostatic energy $U$ because external mechanical work is performed against the attractive Coulomb force $F$).*
3. Partially Filled Dielectric Slab (Thickness $t$, Constant $\kappa$):
$$C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d - t\left(1 - \frac{1}{\kappa}\right)}$$ If a conducting metal plate ($\kappa \to \infty$) is inserted: $$C_{\text{metal}} = \frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d - t}$$

📚 Experimental Manual & Standard Operating Procedure

1. Aim of the Experiment & Apparatus Required

Aim: (a) To study the dependence of capacitance on plate separation ($d$) and overlap area ($A$) and experimentally determine the vacuum permittivity constant ($\varepsilon_0$). (b) To determine the dielectric constant ($\kappa$) of various insulating sheets (Teflon, Paper, Glass, Mica). (c) To contrast energy and voltage behaviors between battery-connected and isolated circuit regimes.

Apparatus: Parallel plate capacitor apparatus with micrometer lead screw, set of dielectric slabs, digital LCR capacitance meter, variable DC power supply, double-pole switch, high-impedance electrometer voltmeter.

2. Step-by-Step Procedure: Permittivity ($\varepsilon_0$) Extraction
  1. Set circuit to Battery Connected ($V = 10.0\text{ V}$) and ensure air dielectric ($\kappa = 1.0$).
  2. Fix plate overlap area at $A = 200.0\text{ cm}^2$ ($0.0200\text{ m}^2$).
  3. Adjust separation $d$ to $2.0, 4.0, 5.0, 8.0, 10.0, 20.0\text{ mm}$ using the distance slider or quick-steppers.
  4. Record capacitance $C$ for each gap and calculate $1/d$.
  5. Plot $C$ vs. $1/d$ on the analytics graph. Calculate slope $m = \varepsilon_0 A$ to extract $\varepsilon_0 = m / A$.
3. Step-by-Step Procedure: Dielectric Testing & Regimes
  1. Fix $d = 10.0\text{ mm}$ and record baseline air capacitance $C_0 \approx 17.71\text{ pF}$.
  2. Slide a Pyrex Glass or Mica slab fully between the plates. Note the immediate increase in capacitance and compute $\kappa = C / C_0$.
  3. Switch to Battery Isolated mode. Increase separation $d$ and observe how voltage $V$ and stored energy $U$ increase linearly with $d$.
4. Precautions & Experimental Errors
  • Lead Capacitance: Stray capacitance of connecting test leads should be zero-nulled on the LCR meter before taking readings.
  • Plate Parallelism: Ensure both plates remain strictly parallel when tightening the micrometer carriage.
  • Humidity & Dust: Keep dielectric sheets dry and free of surface moisture films to prevent surface leakage currents.
