Pile Foundation Load Capacity Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Pile foundations transmit heavy building and bridge loads past weak compressible topsoils down to stiff load-bearing strata. Ultimate capacity ($Q_u = Q_b + Q_s$) is the sum of base end bearing ($Q_b = 9 c_u A_b$) and shaft side shear resistance ($Q_s = \alpha c_u A_s$), divided by a safety factor ($FS = 2.5$) per IS 2911.

📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

Pile foundations transmit heavy building and bridge loads past weak compressible topsoils down to stiff load-bearing strata. Ultimate capacity ($Q_u = Q_b + Q_s$) is the sum of base end bearing ($Q_b = 9 c_u A_b$) and shaft side shear resistance ($Q_s = \alpha c_u A_s$), divided by a safety factor ($FS = 2.5$) per IS 2911.

2. Setting Up the Load Case & Parameters

Adjust the sliders and input fields on the left. Click Calculate & Analyze to run the solvers.

3. Building the Diagrams & Diagnostic Telemetry

The interactive canvas dynamically visualizes stress blocks, shear failure planes, track banking profiles, runway lengths, or pile skin resistance based on live analytical equations.

4. Reading the Theory

6. Design Notes & Standards

All calculations conform to standard Indian Standard codes (IS 456:2000, IRC:37, ICAO Annex 14, Indian Railway Permanent Way Manual, IS 2911, IS 2720).
