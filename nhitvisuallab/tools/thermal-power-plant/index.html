Thermal Power Plant — 3D Isometric Interactive Engineering Simulator

A coal-fired thermal power plant is a complex thermodynamic and electromechanical facility that converts chemical energy in fossil fuel into high-voltage electrical power through the reheat regenerative Rankine cycle. This simulator models all major plant systems in a 3D isometric projection with physical co-relations between fuel feed, combustion temperature, steam generation, mechanical shaft power, electrical generation, heat rejection, and flue gas emission control.

Directly Coupled Steam Turbine & Turbogenerator

In modern utility-scale thermal plants (such as 210 MW to 660 MW units), the multi-stage steam turbine (High Pressure, Intermediate Pressure, and Low Pressure casings) and the synchronous turbogenerator are mounted on a shared rigid horizontal shaft line. High-pressure superheated steam (up to 165 bar and 565 °C) expands across stationary nozzle vanes and rotating rotor blades, developing huge shaft torque at an exact synchronous speed of 3000 RPM (for 50 Hz grids) or 3600 RPM (for 60 Hz grids). The mechanical coupling transmits this mechanical energy directly to the generator rotor, which generates 3-phase AC voltage at 11 kV.

Load Dispatch & Combustion Dynamics

Using the Generation Demand Slider above, you can adjust the electrical setpoint from base load (30 % / 90 MW) to maximum peak load (100 % / 300 MW). The simulator dynamically calculates the required coal feed rate, combustion intensity, furnace flame volume, steam production rate, cooling water heat dissipation, and overall thermal efficiency in real time. Observe the Live Telemetry Graph below the model to watch parameter response curves, settling times, and thermal inertia during load transitions.

📖 How to Use This 3D Simulator

⚡ Generation Load Control

Drag the Generation Demand slider or click the 30% / 70% / 100% preset buttons to throttle plant generation. Watch the boiler flames, steam pipes, turbine rotation, and telemetry traces adapt to the new load point.

📈 Real-Time Telemetry Graph

The rolling time-series graph records live performance parameters. Click any trace toggle pill (Power MW, Boiler Temp, Steam Pressure, Efficiency, Coal Feed) to show or hide specific curves on the chart.

🖴 3D Zoom & Pan

Scroll your mouse wheel anywhere on the 3D plant canvas to smoothly zoom in or out centered at your cursor location. Click and drag with the mouse to pan across the plant.

🔎 Explore Mode & Tooltips

Switch to Explore Mode and click on any numbered callout badge (① Coal, ② Boiler, ③ Ash, ④ Turbine, ⑤ Generator, ⑥ Condenser, ⑦ Cooling Tower, ⑧ ESP/Chimneys, ⑨ Transformer/Grid) to view full engineering descriptions, design specifications, and governing thermodynamic formulas.
