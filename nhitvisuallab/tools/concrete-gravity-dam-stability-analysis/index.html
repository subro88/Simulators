Concrete Gravity Dam Stability Analysis Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Concrete gravity dams maintain stability entirely by their own massive weight resisting overturning and sliding forces. IS 6512 specifies safety factors against overturning ($FSO = \frac{\sum M_R}{\sum M_O} \ge 1.5$) and sliding ($FSS = \frac{\mu \sum V}{\sum H} \ge 1.0$), while ensuring the resultant force falls within the middle third of the base to avoid base tension cracks.
