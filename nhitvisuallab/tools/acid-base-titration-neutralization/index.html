📖 Mathematical Theory & Chemical Equilibrium Engine

WBSCTE Applied Chemistry (BS105/BS109): Neutralization Stoichiometry, Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation, and Speciation $\alpha$-Plots.

1. Volumetric Neutralization Law

At equivalence point, equivalents of acid equal equivalents of base:

$$M_A \cdot V_A \cdot n_A = M_B \cdot V_B \cdot n_B \implies M_{\text{analyte}} = \frac{M_{\text{titrant}} \cdot V_{\text{eq}} \cdot n_{\text{titrant}}}{V_{\text{analyte}} \cdot n_{\text{analyte}}}$$

2. Buffer Region (Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation)

Before equivalence in weak acid titration, $\text{pH}$ is governed by the weak acid / conjugate base ratio:

$$\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a + \log_{10}\left( \frac{[\text{A}^-]}{[\text{HA}]} \right)$$ $$\text{At Half-Equivalence } (V_b = 0.5 V_{\text{eq}}): \quad \mathbf{\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a}$$

3. Polyprotic Triprotic Speciation ($\alpha$-Distribution)

For triprotic acid $\text{H}_3\text{A}$ ($\text{H}_3\text{PO}_4$), fractional species compositions are:

$$\alpha_0 = \frac{[\text{H}^+]^3}{D}, \quad \alpha_1 = \frac{K_{a1}[\text{H}^+]^2}{D}, \quad \alpha_2 = \frac{K_{a1}K_{a2}[\text{H}^+]}{D}, \quad \alpha_3 = \frac{K_{a1}K_{a2}K_{a3}}{D}$$ $$\text{where } D = [\text{H}^+]^3 + K_{a1}[\text{H}^+]^2 + K_{a1}K_{a2}[\text{H}^+] + K_{a1}K_{a2}K_{a3}$$

📚 Comprehensive Experimental Manual & Guidelines

1. Objective & Physical Principles

To standardize unknown acid solutions ($\text{HCl}, \text{CH}_3\text{COOH}, \text{H}_3\text{PO}_4$) using standard $\text{NaOH}$ titrant, master Burette dropwise dispensing, monitor colorimetric indicator transitions, plot potentiometric $\text{pH}$ curves, and analyze polyprotic speciation.

2. Step-by-Step Experimental Procedure
  1. Select a titration system (e.g. $\text{HCl} + \text{NaOH}$ or $\text{CH}_3\text{COOH} + \text{NaOH}$).
  2. Select indicator (e.g. Phenolphthalein). Observe initial colorless flask solution.
  3. Use Fast Stream or Auto Drip to approach the inflection region.
  4. Switch to Single Drop (0.05 mL) near equivalence. Stop at the first faint pink color lasting $\ge 30\text{ s}$.
  5. Record initial ($V_1$) and final ($V_2$) burette readings. Log trials and compute unknown molarity $M_{\text{calc}}$.
