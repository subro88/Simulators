Digital Modulation Schemes Lab (BPSK, QPSK, 16-QAM) — Theory & Governing Equations

Digital modulation schemes map binary data bit streams onto analog sinusoidal carrier states. In QPSK and 16-QAM, symbols combine orthogonal in-phase ($I$) and quadrature ($Q$) carrier components, boosting spectral efficiency ($R_b/BW = k\text{ bps/Hz}$) while maintaining low Bit Error Rate ($BER = Q(\sqrt{2E_b/N_0})$).
