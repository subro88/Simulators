1. Aggregate Impact Value (AIV)

Measures aggregate resistance to sudden shock loading. AIV < 30% is mandatory for wearing surface courses in highways and airport runways.

2. Sand Bulking Mechanism

Surface tension forms a film around damp sand particles, pushing them apart and increasing volume by up to 35% at 4-6% moisture content.

Guided Experiment: Impact Value Determination

1. Subject 350g aggregate sample to 15 hammer blows (13.5 to 14 kg falling 380 mm).
2. Sieve through 2.36 mm IS Sieve and measure mass $W_2$.
3. Calculate $AIV = (W_2 / 350) \times 100\%$.
