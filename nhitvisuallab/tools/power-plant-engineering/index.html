Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S4/PPE Power Plant Engineering

1. Hydroelectric Power Generation

Potential energy of water converted to mechanical energy in turbines: $P = 9.81 \cdot \eta \cdot Q \cdot H\text{ (kW)}$. Turbines: Pelton (High head $>300\text{m}$), Francis (Medium head $30-300\text{m}$), Kaplan (Low head $<30\text{m}$).

2. Thermal Power Station (Rankine Cycle)

Coal pulverized and combusted in boiler $\to$ Superheated high-pressure steam expands in turbine $\to$ Surface condenser condenses steam $\to$ Feed pump recirculates water. Overall thermal efficiency $\approx 35\% - 42\%$.

3. Power Plant Economics & Load Curves

$\text{Load Factor} = \frac{\text{Average Load}}{\text{Peak Maximum Demand}}$, $\text{Diversity Factor} = \frac{\sum \text{Individual Max Demands}}{\text{Coincident System Max Demand}} > 1.0$, $\text{Plant Capacity Factor} = \frac{\text{Actual Energy Produced}}{\text{Rated Capacity} \times 8760\text{h}}$.

4. Nuclear & Non-Conventional Energy

Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR) and Boiling Water Reactor (BWR) using $U^{235}$ fission with control rods (Cadmium/Boron). Solar Photovoltaic (PV) MPPT tracking and Grid-tied Wind turbine generators.
