1. Overview

Concrete mix design according to IS 10262:2019 establishes the optimal, economical proportioning of cement, water, fine aggregate, and coarse aggregate to achieve target mean compressive strength ($f_{ck}' = f_{ck} + 1.65 s$) and required workability.

2. Setting Up the Load Case & Parameters

Use the control sliders on the left to configure survey readings, batch weights, or beam load configurations. Click Calculate & Analyze to run the solvers.

3. Building the Diagrams & Diagnostic Telemetry

The interactive canvas dynamically plots real-time survey profiles, SFD/BMD diagrams, or material stress distributions corresponding to the exact state equations.

4. Reading the Theory

Switch to the Explore & Theory tab to review formal WBSCTE curriculum formulas, derivations, and state equations.

5. Try a Problem

Verify laboratory workshop measurements against simulator predictions to test understanding.

6. Design Notes & Standards

All calculations conform to standard Indian Standard codes (IS 456, IS 10262, IS 3495, IS 269, IS 383, IS 1077).
