Standard operating procedure

  1. Pick a plant module, then use Play / Step / Reset to advance the physics-driven cutaway at a readable speed.
  2. Toggle the frosted Display chip to show or hide particles, labels, the on-canvas equation and the grid.
  3. Open Show calculations for a KaTeX derivation rebuilt from the current operating point; export CSV or a canvas PNG.
  4. Log readings, sweep a parameter, then attempt the nine maintenance tickets, randomized practical calculations and trade quiz.
