Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S3/EMCE Elements of Mechanical Engineering

1. 4-Stroke IC Engine Cycle

Four distinct piston strokes: Suction, Compression, Power/Expansion, and Exhaust over $720^\circ$ (2 revolutions) of crankshaft rotation. Indicated Power $IP = \frac{P_m L A N k}{60 \times 2}$.

2. Steam Turbines

Impulse Turbine (De Laval): Complete steam expansion occurs in stationary nozzles, constant pressure across moving blades.
Reaction Turbine (Parsons): Continuous expansion across both fixed and moving blades.

3. Vapour Compression Refrigeration

Components: Compressor, Condenser, Expansion Valve, and Evaporator. Coefficient of Performance $\text{COP} = \frac{\text{Refrigerating Effect } Q_L}{\text{Work Input } W_{in}}$.

4. Steam Boilers

Fire-tube boilers (Cochran, Lancashire) vs Water-tube boilers (Babcock & Wilcox). Mountings for safety (Safety valve, Water level indicator, Fusible plug).
