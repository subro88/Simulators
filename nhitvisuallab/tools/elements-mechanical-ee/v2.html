Standard operating procedure

  1. Select a plant module and set its process controls; the cutaway animation and thermodynamic charts update together.
  2. For the engine, vary brake load and fuel rack to log IP, BP, FP and mechanical efficiency; use cylinder cut-outs for the Morse test.
  3. For the turbine, compare impulse and reaction speed ratios and watch grid frequency stay at 50 Hz.
  4. For the chiller, log the four P–h state points and compare actual against Carnot COP; apply maintenance fixes to restore motor current.
  5. For the pump, throttle suction until NPSH available falls below required and observe cavitation and vibration.
