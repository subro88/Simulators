Maxwell & Schering AC Bridges Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Four-arm AC bridge networks measure unknown impedances ($L, C, R$) by establishing independent balance conditions for both magnitude and phase. In Maxwell's Bridge, unknown inductance is measured against a standard capacitor ($L_x = R_2 R_3 C_4, R_x = \frac{R_2 R_3}{R_4}$), while Schering's Bridge calculates dielectric loss tangent $\tan\delta = \omega C_4 R_4$.
