RCC Beam Shear Design & Stirrups Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Shear failures in reinforced concrete beams are sudden and catastrophic. Nominal shear stress ($\tau_v = \frac{V_u}{b d}$) is compared against concrete shear capacity ($\tau_c$, IS 456 Table 19). When $\tau_v > \tau_c$, vertical stirrups carrying excess shear ($V_{us} = \frac{0.87 f_y A_{sv} d}{s_v}$) prevent 45° diagonal tension cracking.

1. Overview

Shear failures in reinforced concrete beams are sudden and catastrophic. Nominal shear stress ($\tau_v = \frac{V_u}{b d}$) is compared against concrete shear capacity ($\tau_c$, IS 456 Table 19). When $\tau_v > \tau_c$, vertical stirrups carrying excess shear ($V_{us} = \frac{0.87 f_y A_{sv} d}{s_v}$) prevent 45° diagonal tension cracking.
