8051 Timers & Full-Duplex UART Serial Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

The 8051 microcontroller executes instructions in machine cycles consisting of 12 oscillator clock periods ($T_{MC} = \frac{12}{f_{osc}} = 1.085\ \mu\text{s}$ at $11.0592\text{ MHz}$). Timer 1 in 8-bit auto-reload Mode 2 acts as a hardware baud generator ($TH1 = 256 - \frac{f_{osc}}{384 \times \text{Baud}}$), providing exact standard UART baud rates (9600, 4800, 2400 bps) with 0% timing error.
