1. Overview

Constant-k and m-derived LC ladder filters filter frequency spectra in analog radio and carrier telephony. Constant-k filters exhibit a characteristic impedance $R_0 = \sqrt{L/C}$, while m-derived filter sections introduce infinite attenuation notch frequencies $f_\infty = \frac{f_c}{\sqrt{1-m^2}}$ for steep transition skirts.
