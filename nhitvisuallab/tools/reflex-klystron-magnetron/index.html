Microwave Tubes Lab (Reflex Klystron & Magnetron) — Theory & Governing Equations

Microwave vacuum tubes overcome high-frequency transit time limitations. The Reflex Klystron uses velocity modulation in a single re-entrant resonant cavity and a negative repeller electrode to produce electron bunches returning in phase ($T = (n + \frac{3}{4})T_{RF}$). Cavity Magnetrons employ crossed electric and magnetic fields ($E \times B$) to generate megawatts of pulsed RF power for radar.

Microwave vacuum tubes overcome high-frequency transit time limitations. The Reflex Klystron uses velocity modulation in a single re-entrant resonant cavity and a negative repeller electrode to produce electron bunches returning in phase ($T = (n + \frac{3}{4})T_{RF}$).
