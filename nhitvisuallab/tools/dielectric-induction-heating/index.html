Industrial Heating Lab (Dielectric & Induction) — Theory & Governing Equations

High-frequency industrial heating exploits electromagnetic loss mechanisms. Dielectric heating rapidly cures insulating materials through molecular dipole relaxation ($P = 2\pi f C V^2 \tan\delta$), whereas Induction heating induces eddy currents in surface layers of conductors governed by the electromagnetic skin depth ($\delta = \sqrt{\frac{\rho}{\pi f \mu}}$).
