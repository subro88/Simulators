Transformer Equivalent Circuit & Regulation Lab — Detailed Theory & Mathematics

Single-phase transformers transfer electrical power across voltage levels via mutual electromagnetic induction. The equivalent circuit models winding resistances ($R_{01}$), leakage reactances ($X_{01}$), core magnetizing branch, and computes voltage regulation $VR = \frac{I_1(R_{01}\cos\phi + X_{01}\sin\phi)}{V_1} \times 100\%$.
