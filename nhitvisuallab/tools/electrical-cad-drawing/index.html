Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S4/ED Computer Aided Electrical Drawing

1. 33/11kV Substation SLD

Standard layout includes: Incoming 33kV transmission lines, Surge/Lightning arresters, Isolating switches with earth blades, Instrument transformers (CT/PT), Circuit breakers (SF6/VCB), Power Transformer, and 11kV distribution switchgear.

2. AC Machine Winding Design

Slots per pole per phase $q = \frac{S}{P \cdot m}$. Distribution factor $k_d = \frac{\sin(q\gamma/2)}{q \sin(\gamma/2)}$. Chorded coil pitch factor $k_p = \cos(\alpha/2)$ eliminates 5th and 7th harmonics.

3. DC Armature Lap & Wave Windings

Lap Winding: $A = P$ parallel paths, ideal for high-current, low-voltage machines.
Wave Winding: $A = 2$ parallel paths, ideal for high-voltage, low-current machines.

4. Indian Standard Graphical Symbols (IS 2032)

Standard symbols for Transformers, Circuit Breakers, Isolators, Lightning Arresters, Earthings, Relays, and Motor Starters.
