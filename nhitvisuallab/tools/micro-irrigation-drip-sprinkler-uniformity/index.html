Micro-Irrigation Drip & Sprinkler Uniformity Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Micro-irrigation systems (drip and micro-sprinklers) conserve water by delivering precise applications directly to root zones. Emitter discharge follows $q = k h^x$ (where $x=0$ denotes fully pressure-compensating emitters). Christiansen's Uniformity Coefficient ($CU = 100\left[1 - \frac{\sum |y_i - \bar{y}|}{n \bar{y}}\right] \ge 90\%$) ensures uniform crop water distribution.

📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

Micro-irrigation systems (drip and micro-sprinklers) conserve water by delivering precise applications directly to root zones. Emitter discharge follows $q = k h^x$ (where $x=0$ denotes fully pressure-compensating emitters). Christiansen's Uniformity Coefficient ($CU = 100\left[1 - \frac{\sum |y_i - \bar{y}|}{n \bar{y}}\right] \ge 90\%$) ensures uniform crop water distribution.
