Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S6/CEM Control of Electrical Machines

1. Star-Delta Starting Method

Phase voltage in Star is $\frac{V_L}{\sqrt{3}} \approx 58\%$. Starting current $I_{star} = \frac{1}{3} I_{DOL}$ and Starting torque $T_{star} = \frac{1}{3} T_{DOL}$. Transition timer switches contactors at $\approx 80\%$ rated speed.

2. Direct-on-Line (DOL) vs Auto-Transformer

DOL starters apply full voltage with $5-7\times$ inrush current (suitable for $<5\text{HP}$). Auto-transformer starters offer tapping options ($50\%, 65\%, 80\%$) where $I_{line} = x^2 I_{DOL}$.

3. Slip-Ring Rotor Resistance Starters

External 3-step resistance bank connected to rotor slip rings maximizes starting torque ($T_{start} \propto \frac{R_2}{\sqrt{R_2^2 + X_2^2}}$) while simultaneously suppressing starting current.

4. Dynamic & Plugging Electric Braking

Dynamic Braking: AC supply disconnected and DC current injected into stator to produce stationary braking flux.
Plugging: Reversing any two stator phases produces rapid reverse torque.
