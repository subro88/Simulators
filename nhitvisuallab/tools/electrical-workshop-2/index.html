Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S6/EWII Electrical Workshop - II

1. AC Motor Stator Rewinding Steps

Stripping old burnt coils, cleaning slots, slot lining with Class F Nomex/Mylar composite paper, winding coils on former rig, insertion into slots, bamboo/FRP wedging, phase-to-phase insulation, and overhang varnishing with bake curing.

2. Coil Pitch & Distribution Factor

Chorded (short-pitch) coils reduce copper overhang weight and eliminate unwanted 5th and 7th harmonic voltages: $k_p = \cos(\alpha/2)$.

3. Armature Commutator Drop Test

DC millivolt drop test between adjacent commutator segments using low voltage DC supply. Uniform millivolt drop confirms healthy coils without short circuits or open joints.

4. High Voltage Cable Jointing & End Termination

Heat-shrinkable and cold-shrinkable termination kits for $11\text{kV}/33\text{kV}$ XLPE cables, stress control tubing, and crimping copper/aluminium lugs.
