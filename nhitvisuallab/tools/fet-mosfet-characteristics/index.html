📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

Field Effect Transistors (FETs) are voltage-controlled unipolar devices offering near-infinite input impedance. In the saturation region, drain current follows Shockley's parabolic law $I_D = I_{DSS}\left(1 - \frac{V_{GS}}{V_P}\right)^2$, enabling linear small-signal amplification.

2. Setting Up the Load Case & Parameters

Use the control sliders on the left to configure circuit variables. Click Calculate & Simulate to trigger real-time Python/JS solvers.

3. Building the Diagrams & Diagnostic Telemetry

The interactive canvas dynamically plots real-time waveforms, frequency responses, or load characteristics corresponding to the exact state equations.

4. Reading the Theory

Switch to the Explore & Theory tab to review formal WBSCTE curriculum formulas, derivations, and state equations.

5. Try a Problem

Verify laboratory workshop measurements against simulator predictions to test understanding.

6. Design Notes & Standards

All calculations conform to standard electrical and electronics engineering practices (IEEE, IEC, IS 2026, IS 4722).
