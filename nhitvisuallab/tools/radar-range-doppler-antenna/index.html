Radar Range Equation, Doppler & Antenna Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Radar (Radio Detection and Ranging) calculates target range by pulse echo time delay ($R = \frac{c t}{2}$) and radial velocity via the Doppler frequency shift ($f_d = \frac{2 v_r f_0}{c}$). The fundamental Radar Range Equation establishes $R_{max} = \left[\frac{P_t G^2 \lambda^2 \sigma}{(4\pi)^3 P_{min}}\right]^{1/4}$, scaling with the 4th-root of peak transmitter power.
