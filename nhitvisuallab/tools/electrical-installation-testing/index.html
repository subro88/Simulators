Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S6/EIMT Electrical Installation, Maintenance & Testing

1. Transformer Sumpner (Back-to-Back) Test

Two identical transformers are connected with primaries in parallel and secondaries in phase opposition. Full load copper and iron losses are measured simultaneously with minimal energy drawn from the supply grid.

2. Earth Resistance (Fall of Potential Method)

Three-terminal earth tester ($E, P, C$). Current injected into auxiliary current electrode $C$; potential measured at auxiliary electrode $P$ placed at $61.8\%$ of distance $E-C$. Measured $R_e = \frac{V_{EP}}{I}$.

3. Dielectric Absorption & Polarization Index (PI)

$\text{PI} = \frac{R_{10\text{min}}}{R_{1\text{min}}}$. $\text{PI} > 2.0$ indicates dry, uncontaminated insulation free of moisture tracking.

4. Statutory Inspection & IE Rules

Mandatory clearances for transformers, switchgear, busbars, and safety permit-to-work (PTW) procedures.
