Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S4/PFII Electrical Maintenance & Practice

1. Transformer Oil Testing (IS 6792)

Dielectric breakdown voltage (BDV) indicates oil purity and moisture content. New untreated oil must withstand $\ge 30\text{kV}$, filtered treated oil $\ge 60\text{kV}$. Other tests: Acidity, Tan delta, Flash point, and Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA).

2. Insulation Resistance (Megger Test)

IEEE 43 standard: Minimum insulation resistance $R_{ins} = kV + 1\text{ M}\Omega$. Polarization Index ($PI = \frac{R_{10\text{min}}}{R_{1\text{min}}}$): $PI \ge 2.0$ indicates clean, dry insulation.

3. Earth Pit Maintenance

Periodic watering with saline/charcoal slurry. Earth grid resistance $R \le 1.0\Omega$ for substations and $R \le 5.0\Omega$ for domestic consumer installations.

4. Substation Preventive Maintenance

Thermographic infrared scanning for hot joints, Buchholz relay gas release check, silica gel breather color (Blue $\to$ Dry, Pink $\to$ Moisture saturated).
