Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S4/EMC Electrical Measurement & Control

1. LVDT Inductive Transducer

Linear Variable Differential Transformer converts mechanical displacement into a differential AC voltage ($V_{out} = V_{S1} - V_{S2}$). Null position yields $V_{out} \approx 0\text{V}$.

2. PID Controller Response

Proportional ($K_p$): Reduces rise time and steady-state error.
Integral ($K_i$): Eliminates steady-state offset ($e_{ss} \to 0$).
Derivative ($K_d$): Increases damping and reduces peak overshoot.

3. Time-Domain Specifications

Peak overshoot $\%M_p = 100 e^{-\frac{\zeta\pi}{\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}}}$, Settling time $t_s = \frac{4}{\zeta\omega_n}$, Rise time $t_r = \frac{\pi - \beta}{\omega_d}$.

4. Industrial Transducers

Thermocouple (Seebeck effect), RTD Pt100 ($R_t = R_0(1+\alpha t)$), Strain Gauge (Gauge factor $GF = \frac{\Delta R/R}{\Delta L/L}$), Piezoelectric quartz crystals.
