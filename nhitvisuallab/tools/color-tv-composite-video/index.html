Color TV & Composite Video Signal Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

In broadcast television engineering (PAL 625-line standard), the Composite Video Baseband Signal (CVBS) encapsulates luminance $Y = 0.30 R + 0.59 G + 0.11 B$, color difference subcarriers $U = 0.493(B-Y)$ and $V = 0.877(R-Y)$ modulated onto a $4.433\text{ MHz}$ subcarrier, alongside horizontal and vertical synchronizing and blanking pulses.
