Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S6/EDEC Electrical Design, Estimation & Costing

1. Indian Electricity (IE) Rules & NEC

Rule 28: Low, Medium, High and Extra High Voltage ratings.
Rule 46: Periodic inspection of installations.
Rule 54: Declared voltage limits ($\pm 6\%$ for low/medium voltage, $\pm 12.5\%$ for extra high voltage).
Rule 77: Minimum ground clearances for overhead lines.

2. Sub-circuit Design Standards (IS 732)

Lighting Sub-circuit: Maximum load $800\text{W}$ or $10$ points per circuit.
Power Sub-circuit: Maximum load $3000\text{W}$ or $2$ socket outlets per circuit.
Maximum allowable feeder voltage drop $\le 3.0\%$ from consumer intake point.

3. Cable Sizing & Derating Factors

Current rating modified by: Ambient temperature factor $k_t$, Grouping factor $k_g$, and Thermal soil resistivity $k_s$. Required $I_{rated} = \frac{I_{fl}}{k_t \cdot k_g \cdot k_s}$.

4. Earth Electrode Resistance (IS 3043)

Pipe and plate earthing with charcoal and salt layers. Substation earth resistance $\le 1.0\Omega$, industrial $\le 2.0\Omega$, domestic consumer $\le 5.0\Omega$.
