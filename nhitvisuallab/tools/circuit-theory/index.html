Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S3/CTN Circuit Theory & Networks

1. Network Theorems

Thevenin's Theorem: Linear two-terminal circuit replaced by $V_{th}$ in series with $R_{th}$.
Maximum Power Transfer: Maximum power delivered to load when $R_L = R_{th}$, giving $P_{max} = \frac{V_{th}^2}{4 R_{th}}$.

2. Series RLC AC Resonance

At resonant frequency $f_r = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}$, inductive reactance cancels capacitive reactance ($X_L = X_C$). Impedance is minimized ($Z = R$), current is maximized, and power factor is unity ($\cos \phi = 1$).

3. Quality Factor & Bandwidth

Quality factor $Q = \frac{\omega_0 L}{R} = \frac{1}{R}\sqrt{\frac{L}{C}}$ defines voltage magnification and selectivity. Half-power bandwidth $BW = f_2 - f_1 = \frac{f_r}{Q}$.

4. Star-Delta Transformation

Three-phase network equivalence: $R_{ab} = R_a + R_b + \frac{R_a R_b}{R_c}$ and $R_a = \frac{R_{ab} R_{ca}}{R_{ab} + R_{bc} + R_{ca}}$.
