Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE ME/S3/TE1 Steam Properties & Mollier Diagram Lab

1. Steam Thermodynamics Definitions

Sensible Heat ($h_f$): Heat required to raise $1\text{ kg}$ water from $0^\circ\text{C}$ to $T_{sat}$.
Latent Heat ($h_{fg}$): Heat required to evaporate $1\text{ kg}$ saturated water into steam.
Wet Steam Enthalpy: $h = h_f + x \cdot h_{fg}$.

2. Throttling Calorimeter

In an isenthalpic throttling process ($h_1 = h_2$), wet steam passes through an orifice to atmospheric pressure where it superheats: $h_{f1} + x_1 h_{fg1} = h_{g2} + c_{ps} (T_{sup2} - T_{sat2})$.

3. Mollier Diagram (h-s Chart)

Enthalpy ($h$) vs Entropy ($s$) diagram used for turbine expansion, nozzle design, and dryness determination without repetitive steam table lookups.
