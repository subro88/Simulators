RCC Doubly Reinforced Beam Design Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Doubly reinforced beams are designed when architectural headroom restricts beam dimensions such that the applied moment exceeds the limiting singly reinforced capacity ($M_u > M_{u,lim}$). Compression steel ($A_{sc}$) carries the excess moment ($M_{u2} = (f_{sc} - f_{cc}) A_{sc} (d - d')$), while also reducing long-term creep and shrinkage deflections.
