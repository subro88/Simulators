Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S5/ECA Energy Conservation & Audit

1. Power Factor Correction Economics

$\text{Capacitor Rating (kVAR)} = P_{\text{kW}} (\tan \phi_1 - \tan \phi_2)$. Improves voltage regulation, eliminates low-PF surcharge penalty, and frees up transformer substation capacity.

2. Sankey Energy Balance Diagram

Visual map of energy inputs, useful work output, and parasitic losses (exhaust flue gas heat, cooling water losses, pipe friction, electrical cable resistance).

3. Energy Efficient Electric Motors (IE3/IE4)

Thinner high-grade silicon steel laminations reduce iron core losses, thicker copper slot conductors minimize $I^2 R$ stator losses, and aerodynamic fan reduces windage.

4. Financial Payback & Life Cycle Costing

$\text{Simple Payback Period} = \frac{\text{Capital Investment}}{\text{Annual Net Cost Savings}}$. Net Present Value ($NPV$) and Internal Rate of Return ($IRR$) for industrial retrofit projects.
