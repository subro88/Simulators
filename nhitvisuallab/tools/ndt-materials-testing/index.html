Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE ME/S3/MEM Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Lab

1. Ultrasonic Testing (UT) Principles

High-frequency acoustic sound waves ($1-10\text{ MHz}$) propagate through material. Acoustic impedance $Z = \rho \cdot v$. Reflections occur at material boundaries and internal defects: $d = \frac{v \cdot t}{2}$.

2. Radiographic Testing (RT)

Differential absorption of X-rays or Gamma rays ($^{192}\text{Ir}, ^{60}\text{Co}$) based on density: $I = I_0 e^{-\mu x}$.

3. Magnetic Particle Testing (MPI)

Detects surface and near-surface cracks in ferromagnetic materials using magnetic flux leakage and iron oxide powder.

4. Liquid Penetrant Testing (DPI)

Capillary action draws low surface tension penetrant dye into open surface-breaking cracks.
