IS 800 Steel Bolted & Welded Connections Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

IS 800:2007 Limit State Method dictates structural steel connection design. For bearing-type bolts, design shear strength ($V_{dsb} = \frac{f_{ub}}{\sqrt{3}\gamma_{mb}} A_{nb}$) and bearing strength ($V_{dpb} = \frac{2.5 k_b d t f_u}{\gamma_{mb}}$) determine bolt value. Fillet welds are sized by effective throat thickness ($t_t = 0.707 s$) and design shear strength ($f_{wd} = \frac{f_u}{\sqrt{3}\gamma_{mw}}$).
