Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S4/ADE Applied & Digital Electronics

1. NE555 Timer Astable Multivibrator

Charge time $t_H = 0.693(R_A + R_B)C$, Discharge time $t_L = 0.693 R_B C$. Oscillation frequency $f = \frac{1.44}{(R_A + 2R_B)C}$ and Duty cycle $D = \frac{R_A + R_B}{R_A + 2R_B} \times 100\%$.

2. JK Flip-Flops & Counters

Master-slave JK flip-flops eliminate race-around conditions when $J=K=1$. Modulo-N asynchronous ripple counters cascade clock pulses through toggle stages ($T_{clock} = 2^N$).

3. Operational Amplifier Applications

Virtual ground concept ($V_+ = V_-$). Inverting Amplifier $A_v = -\frac{R_f}{R_1}$, Non-inverting $A_v = 1 + \frac{R_f}{R_1}$, Active Sallen-Key low-pass filter with $-40\text{dB/decade}$ rolloff.

4. Data Converters (ADC & DAC)

R-2R Ladder DAC output voltage $V_{out} = -V_{ref} \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{b_i}{2^i}$. Successive Approximation Register (SAR) ADC conversion speed $n$ clock cycles.
