Airport Runway Length Corrections Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

The basic runway length ($L_0$) specified under standard sea-level atmospheric conditions ($15^\circ\text{C}, 760\text{ mm Hg}$) must be corrected for actual site conditions: 1) Elevation (+7% per 300m elevation above MSL), 2) Airport Reference Temperature (+1% per 1°C rise in ART above standard atmosphere), and 3) Effective Gradient (+20% per 1% effective gradient per ICAO).

📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

The basic runway length ($L_0$) specified under standard sea-level atmospheric conditions ($15^\circ\text{C}, 760\text{ mm Hg}$) must be corrected for actual site conditions: 1) Elevation (+7% per 300m elevation above MSL), 2) Airport Reference Temperature (+1% per 1°C rise in ART above standard atmosphere), and 3) Effective Gradient (+20% per 1% effective gradient per ICAO).

2. Setting Up the Load Case & Parameters

Adjust the sliders and input fields on the left. Click Calculate & Analyze to run the solvers.

3. Building the Diagrams & Diagnostic Telemetry

The interactive canvas dynamically visualizes stress blocks, shear failure planes, track banking profiles, runway lengths, or pile skin resistance based on live analytical equations.

4. Reading the Theory

Switch to the Explore & Theory tab to review formal WBSCTE curriculum formulas, derivations, and state equations.

5. Try a Problem

Verify laboratory workshop measurements against simulator predictions to test understanding.

6. Design Notes & Standards

All calculations conform to standard Indian Standard codes (IS 456:2000, IRC:37, ICAO Annex 14, Indian Railway Permanent Way Manual, IS 2911, IS 2720).
