Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE ME/S3/TE1 Air Standard Thermodynamic Cycles Lab

1. Otto Cycle (Constant Volume Heat Addition)

Efficiency $\eta_{otto} = 1 - \frac{1}{r^{\gamma - 1}}$. Process 1-2: Isentropic compression, 2-3: Reversible constant volume heat addition, 3-4: Isentropic expansion, 4-1: Constant volume heat rejection.

2. Diesel Cycle (Constant Pressure Heat Addition)

Efficiency $\eta_{diesel} = 1 - \frac{1}{r^{\gamma - 1}} \left[\frac{r_c^\gamma - 1}{\gamma (r_c - 1)}\right]$. For same compression ratio, $\eta_{otto} > \eta_{diesel}$.

3. Mean Effective Pressure (MEP)

$P_{mep} = \frac{W_{net}}{V_1 - V_2} = \frac{W_{net}}{V_s}$, representing hypothetical constant pressure producing the same net work output.

4. Carnot Cycle Limitations

Highest theoretical efficiency $\eta = 1 - \frac{T_L}{T_H}$, but impossible practically due to extremely high pressure and very low mean effective pressure.
