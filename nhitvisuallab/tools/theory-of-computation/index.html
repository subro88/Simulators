Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE CST/5/504 Theory of Computation

1. Finite Automata (DFA vs NFA)

5-tuple $(Q, \Sigma, \delta, q_0, F)$. DFA transition $\delta: Q \times \Sigma \to Q$. NFA transition $\delta: Q \times \Sigma \to 2^Q$. Subset construction algorithm converts any NFA to an equivalent DFA.

2. Regular Expressions & Pumping Lemma

Equivalence with Regular Languages via Arden's Theorem. Pumping Lemma ($w = xyz, |xy| \le p, |y| \ge 1, xy^i z \in L$) proves non-regularity of languages such as $a^n b^n$.

3. Context-Free Grammars (CFG) & PDA

Chomsky Hierarchy Type-2. Pushdown Automata (PDA) add an auxiliary LIFO stack memory. Chomsky Normal Form ($A \to BC$ or $A \to a$) enables $O(n^3)$ CYK parsing.

4. Turing Machines & Computability

Type-0 recursively enumerable languages. Church-Turing Thesis and Halting Problem undecidability proof via diagonal argument.
