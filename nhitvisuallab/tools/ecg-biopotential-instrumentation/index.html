ECG Biopotential Instrumentation Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Electrocardiography (ECG) measures the rhythmic electrical depolarization and repolarization biopotentials of the human myocardium. Einthoven's Triangle law dictates that $Lead\ II = Lead\ I + Lead\ III$, while 3-Op-Amp differential instrumentation amplifiers with $> 100\text{ dB}$ CMRR and Right Leg Drive (RLD) circuits eliminate hazardous 50Hz common-mode power line interference.

📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

Electrocardiography (ECG) measures the rhythmic electrical depolarization and repolarization biopotentials of the human myocardium. Einthoven's Triangle law dictates that $Lead\ II = Lead\ I + Lead\ III$, while 3-Op-Amp differential instrumentation amplifiers with $> 100\text{ dB}$ CMRR and Right Leg Drive (RLD) circuits eliminate hazardous 50Hz common-mode power line interference.
