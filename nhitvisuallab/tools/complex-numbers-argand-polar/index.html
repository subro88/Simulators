📚 Complex Analysis, Argand Plane & Conformal Mapping Foundations

1. Dual Representations & Euler's Identity

A complex number $z \in \mathbb{C}$ possesses three equivalent algebraic and geometric formulations:

$$z = x + iy \quad \text{(Rectangular / Cartesian Form)}$$ $$z = r(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta) = r e^{i\theta} \quad \text{(Polar & Euler Exponential Form)}$$ $$r = |z| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}, \quad \theta = \text{Arg}(z) = \text{atan2}(y, x) \in (-\pi, \pi]$$

2. Vector Operations & Triangle Inequality

Complex addition mirrors the Parallelogram Law of Vectors in $\mathbb{R}^2$:

$$z_1 + z_2 = (x_1 + x_2) + i(y_1 + y_2)$$ $$||z_1| - |z_2|| \le |z_1 + z_2| \le |z_1| + |z_2| \quad \text{(Triangle Inequality)}$$

3. Multiplication as Dilative Rotation & de Moivre's Theorem

Multiplying two complex numbers multiplies their moduli and adds their arguments:

$$z_1 \cdot z_2 = (r_1 r_2) e^{i(\theta_1 + \theta_2)}$$ $$z^n = r^n [\cos(n\theta) + i\sin(n\theta)] = r^n e^{i n \theta} \quad \text{(de Moivre's Theorem)}$$

4. Roots of Unity & Regular Inscribed Polygons

The $n$-th roots of a complex number $z = r e^{i\theta}$ form a regular $n$-sided polygon inscribed in a concentric circle of radius $R = \sqrt[n]{r}$:

$$w_k = \sqrt[n]{r} \exp\left( i \frac{\theta + 2k\pi}{n} \right) \quad \text{for } k = 0, 1, \dots, n-1$$ $$\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} w_k = 0 \quad \text{(Sum of Roots Identity)}$$

5. Conformal Mappings & Cauchy-Riemann Conditions

An analytic function $w = f(z) = u(x, y) + i v(x, y)$ preserves both the magnitude and sense of angles at all points where $f'(z) \ne 0$:

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}, \quad \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = -\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} \quad \text{(Cauchy-Riemann Equations)}$$ $$w = \frac{az + b}{cz + d} \quad (ad - bc \ne 0) \quad \text{(General Möbius Transformation)}$$

📗 Standard Operating Procedure & Student Exploration Manual
  1. Interactive Pointer Dragging: Left-click and drag the blue $z_1$ node or emerald $z_2$ node anywhere on the Argand plane. The coordinate sliders and Euler formulas update in real time.
  2. Vector Arithmetic: Select an operation from the top toolbar (Addition, Multiplication, Powers, etc.). The amber/crimson resultant vector $z_R$ updates dynamically with ghost construction lines.
  3. Verify Triangle Inequality: Observe the Triangle Inequality ledger right below the expression cards. Drag $z_1$ and $z_2$ to align collinearly to witness the equality condition $|z_1 + z_2| = |z_1| + |z_2|$.
  4. de Moivre & Roots of Unity: Switch to Roots mode, adjust slider $n$, and observe the $n$ roots forming the vertices of a regular inscribed polygon while verifying $\sum w_k = 0$.
  5. Conformal Mapping Exploration: Switch to the Conformal Mapping tab, select Möbius, Quadratic, or Joukowsky transform, drag test probe $z_0$, and observe that the orthogonal angle needles in the $w$-plane remain strictly $90^\circ$.
