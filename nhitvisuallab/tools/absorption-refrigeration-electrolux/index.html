1. Overview

The Electrolux 3-fluid vapor absorption refrigerator operates without any mechanical pump or moving parts, utilizing a bubble pump and hydrogen gas. This simulator evaluates Dalton's law of partial pressures ($P_{total} = P_{NH3} + P_{H2} = 15 \text{ bar}$), low-temperature ammonia evaporation, and maximum theoretical Carnot COP.
