4-Loop Pressurised Water Reactor (PWR) — Process Operation & 3D Virtual Tour

In a 4-Loop Pressurised Water Reactor (PWR) (such as the Westinghouse 4-loop or Sizewell B design), four independent primary coolant loops extract thermal energy from a single reactor core. High-pressure water at 155 bar circulates through the Reactor Pressure Vessel (RPV) and four vertical U-tube Steam Generators, driving a tandem-compound turbine generator train with three low-pressure turbines and sea-cooled condensers.

Primary Circuit & Pressuriser Dynamics

Water enters the reactor core at 292 °C and exits at 325 °C. The top-mounted Coolant Pressuriser maintains primary pressure above saturation to eliminate core boiling. Four vertical Reactor Coolant Pumps (RCPs) deliver over 95,000 m³/hr of total coolant flow.

Secondary Steam Circuit & Sea Cooling

Each of the four Steam Generators contains thousands of Inconel U-tubes. Clean secondary water absorbs heat through the tube walls and boils into dry saturated steam at ~65 bar. Steam from all four generators combines in the main steam manifold, expands across the High Pressure Turbine and three double-flow Low Pressure Turbines, and discharges into the titanium Steam Condenser cooled by direct sea water intake.
