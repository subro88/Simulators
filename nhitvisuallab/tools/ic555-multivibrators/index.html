IC 555 Timer Multivibrators Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

The NE555 monolithic timer IC contains two precision voltage comparators referenced to $\frac{1}{3} V_{CC}$ and $\frac{2}{3} V_{CC}$, an RS latch, and an internal discharge transistor. In astable mode, it generates square waves with frequency $f = \frac{1.44}{(R_A + 2 R_B) C}$ and duty cycle $D = \frac{R_A + R_B}{R_A + 2 R_B}$, while monostable mode produces one-shot pulses of duration $T = 1.1 R_A C$.
