Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE ME/S3/MEM Heat Treatment of Steels Virtual Lab

1. Major Heat Treatment Processes

Full Annealing: Heat to $30-50^\circ\text{C}$ above $A_3$, soak, slow furnace cool ($10-30^\circ\text{C/hr}$). Maximum softness and stress relief.
Normalizing: Air cooling from above $A_3$ to yield uniform fine pearlite.
Hardening: Rapid water/oil quench to form brittle BCT Martensite.
Tempering: Reheating quenched steel below $A_1$ ($150-650^\circ\text{C}$) to recover toughness.

2. TTT & CCT Diagrams

Time-Temperature-Transformation (Isothermal) and Continuous Cooling Transformation curves define the critical cooling rate required to avoid the pearlite/bainite nose.

3. Case Hardening Techniques

Carburizing, Nitriding, Cyaniding, and Induction hardening for producing high surface wear resistance with a ductile tough core.
