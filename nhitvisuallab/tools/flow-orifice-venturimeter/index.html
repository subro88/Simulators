Orifice Meter & Venturimeter Flow Lab — Detailed Theory & Mathematics

Venturimeters and orifice meters are differential pressure flow meters applying Bernoulli's conservation of energy principle along a streamline. This virtual lab models differential manometer conversion $H = h(\frac{S_m}{S_f}-1)$, theoretical vs actual discharge calculations, coefficient of discharge $C_d$, and pipe Reynolds numbers.
