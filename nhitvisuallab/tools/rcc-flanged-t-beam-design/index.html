RCC Flanged T-Beam Design Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Flanged T-beams leverage the high compressive strength of cast-in-place floor slabs as wide compressive flanges. The effective flange width ($b_f = \frac{l_0}{6} + b_w + 6 D_f$ for continuous beams) significantly increases flexural resistance compared to standalone rectangular beams of stem width $b_w$.

📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

Flanged T-beams leverage the high compressive strength of cast-in-place floor slabs as wide compressive flanges. The effective flange width ($b_f = \frac{l_0}{6} + b_w + 6 D_f$ for continuous beams) significantly increases flexural resistance compared to standalone rectangular beams of stem width $b_w$.

3. Building the Diagrams & Diagnostic Telemetry

6. Design Notes & Standards

All calculations conform to standard Indian Standard codes (IS 456:2000, IRC:37, ICAO Annex 14, Indian Railway Permanent Way Manual, IS 2911, IS 2720).
