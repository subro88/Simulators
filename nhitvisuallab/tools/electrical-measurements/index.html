Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S3/EMI Electrical Measuring Instruments

1. PMMC vs Moving Iron (MI) Meters

PMMC: DC only, linear scale, high sensitivity, Eddy current damping ($T_d = NBAI$).
Moving Iron: AC & DC, square-law non-linear scale ($T_d = \frac{1}{2}I^2 \frac{dL}{d\theta}$), Air friction damping.

2. Two-Wattmeter 3-Phase Power

Total Active Power $P = W_1 + W_2$. Power factor angle $\tan \phi = \sqrt{3} \frac{W_1 - W_2}{W_1 + W_2}$. Special cases: $\cos \phi = 1 \implies W_1 = W_2$; $\cos \phi = 0.5 \implies W_2 = 0$; $\cos \phi = 0 \implies W_1 = -W_2$.

3. AC Measurement Bridges

Maxwell's Inductance Bridge: Measures medium-Q inductors ($L_x = R_2 R_3 C_4, R_x = \frac{R_2 R_3}{R_4}$).
Schering Bridge: Measures capacitance and dielectric loss angle $\tan \delta$.

4. Instrument Transformers

Current Transformers (CT, secondary 5A) and Potential Transformers (PT, secondary 110V) for high voltage/current isolation and safety.
