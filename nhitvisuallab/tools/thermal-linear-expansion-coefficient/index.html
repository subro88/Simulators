📖 Mathematical Theory & Timoshenko Bimetal Formulations

WBSCTE Applied Physics-I (BS103/BS107): Linear Expansion, Differential Strain, and Beam Curvature.

1. Timoshenko Radius of Curvature ($R$)

For two bonded metal layers with thermal expansion coefficients $\alpha_1, \alpha_2$ ($\alpha_1 > \alpha_2$), total thickness $t = t_1 + t_2$, subjected to temperature change $\Delta T = T - T_0$:

$$R = \frac{2 t}{3 (\alpha_1 - \alpha_2) \Delta T}$$ $$\kappa = \frac{1}{R} = \frac{3 (\alpha_1 - \alpha_2) \Delta T}{2 t}$$

2. Free-End Tip Deflection ($\delta$)

The vertical tip displacement $\delta$ of a cantilever beam of length $L$ under curvature $\kappa$ is:

$$\delta = R \left[ 1 - \cos\left(\frac{L}{R}\right) \right] \approx \frac{L^2}{2R} = \frac{3 L^2 (\alpha_1 - \alpha_2) \Delta T}{4 t}$$

3. Thermostat Switching Temperature ($T_{\text{trigger}}$)

Setting tip deflection equal to the contact screw gap $\delta = d_{\text{gap}}$ yields the exact cutoff temperature:

$$T_{\text{trigger}} = T_0 + \frac{4 t d_{\text{gap}}}{3 L^2 (\alpha_1 - \alpha_2)}$$

📚 Comprehensive Experimental Manual & Guidelines

1. Objective & Physical Principles

To investigate thermal expansion in bimetallic laminated beams (Brass/Invar, Aluminium/Steel, Copper/Steel), verify Timoshenko's curvature equation $\kappa = \frac{3\Delta\alpha\Delta T}{2t}$, calibrate contact screw gap threshold $d_{\text{gap}}$, and demonstrate closed-loop thermostat switching.

2. Step-by-Step Experimental Procedure
  1. Select a metal pair preset (e.g. Brass/Invar, $\Delta\alpha = 17.8 \times 10^{-6}/\text{K}$).
  2. Set beam dimensions ($L = 150\text{ mm}$, $t = 1.5\text{ mm}$) and contact gap $d_{\text{gap}} = 2.5\text{ mm}$.
  3. Vary temperature $T$ from $20^\circ\text{C}$ to $100^\circ\text{C}$. Observe beam bending downward away from Brass.
  4. Identify the exact temperature at which tip deflection $\delta = 2.50\text{ mm}$ opens the circuit ($T_{\text{trigger}} = 66.8^\circ\text{C}$).
  5. Click Cryo Spray to observe reverse sub-zero curvature ($\Delta T < 0$).
  6. Log trial data into the Observation Table and export CSV for analysis.
