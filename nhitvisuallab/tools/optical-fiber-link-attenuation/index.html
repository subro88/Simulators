Optical Fiber Link Attenuation & NA Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Optical fibers guide optical signals through dielectric Total Internal Reflection (TIR) when incident angles exceed the critical angle ($\theta_c = \arcsin(n_2/n_1)$). The Numerical Aperture ($NA = \sqrt{n_1^2 - n_2^2}$) defines the light gathering capability, while the Normalized V-Number ($V = \frac{2\pi a}{\lambda}NA$) dictates single-mode ($V < 2.405$) or multi-mode operation.
