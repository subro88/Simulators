📖 Mechanical Principles & Mathematical Derivations Syllabus Reference ES102/ES104

1. Apparatus Geometry & Kinematic Velocity Ratio (\(VR\))

A worm and worm wheel lifting machine consists of a driving steel screw (Worm) of pitch diameter \(d_w\) and number of starts \(n\), meshing perpendicularly with a toothed gear wheel (Worm Wheel) possessing \(T\) teeth. The worm is driven by an effort pulley/wheel of radius \(R\), while the worm wheel is keyed rigidly to a coaxial load drum of radius \(r\).

$$\text{Distance moved by effort in 1 revolution } (y) = 2 \pi R$$ $$\text{Angular advance of worm wheel in 1 worm rev } = \frac{n}{T} \text{ revolutions}$$ $$\text{Linear lift of suspended load } (x) = \left(\frac{n}{T}\right) \cdot 2 \pi r$$ $$VR = \frac{y}{x} = \frac{2 \pi R}{\left(\frac{n}{T}\right) 2 \pi r} = \left(\frac{R}{r}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{T}{n}\right) = \left(\frac{D_{\text{effort}}}{D_{\text{drum}}}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{T}{n}\right)$$

2. Screw Thread Tribology & Self-Locking Boundary (\(\eta \le 50\%\))

The axial lead \(L_w\) and screw lead angle \(\lambda\) are governed by the pitch \(p\) and pitch diameter \(d_w\):

$$L_w = n \cdot p, \qquad \tan\lambda = \frac{L_w}{\pi \cdot d_w} = \frac{n \cdot p}{\pi \cdot d_w}$$ $$\eta_{\text{mesh}} = \frac{\tan\lambda}{\tan(\lambda + \phi)}, \quad \text{where } \tan\phi = \mu \text{ (Friction Angle)}$$

Irreversible Self-Locking Criterion: A lifting machine is strictly self-locking (will not drop or back-drive when the input effort is removed) if and only if its mechanical efficiency is less than or equal to 50%:

$$\eta \le 50\% \iff \lambda \le \phi \iff \tan\lambda \le \mu$$
  • Single-Start Worm (\(n=1\)): Very small lead angle (\(\lambda \approx 3^\circ - 5^\circ\)) \(\implies \lambda < \phi\). The machine is Strictly Self-Locking (safe overhead hoist).
  • Quad-Start Worm (\(n=4\)): Steep lead angle (\(\lambda \approx 15^\circ - 20^\circ\)) \(\implies \lambda > \phi\). The machine is Overhauling / Reversible (unsafe without an external ratchet or friction brake).

3. Law of the Machine & Asymptotic Limits

$$P = m \cdot W + C$$ $$MA = \frac{W}{P} = \frac{W}{m W + C} = \frac{1}{m + \frac{C}{W}} \implies MA_{\text{max}} = \lim_{W \to \infty} MA = \frac{1}{m}$$ $$\eta = \frac{MA}{VR} \times 100\% = \frac{1}{(m W + C) \cdot VR} \cdot W \times 100\% \implies \eta_{\text{max}} = \frac{1}{m \cdot VR} \times 100\%$$

📚 Comprehensive User Guide & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. Apparatus Overview & Mechanical Principle
A Worm and Worm Wheel is an industrial hoisting machine utilizing a threaded steel screw gearing into a toothed wheel to achieve massive single-stage gear reduction with compact non-intersecting perpendicular shafts. In single-start configurations (\(n=1\)), the lead angle \(\lambda\) is smaller than the friction angle \(\phi\), providing fail-safe Irreversible Self-Locking where the load cannot back-drive the machine when effort is released.
2. Experimental Protocol & Multi-Load Sweeps
  1. Record initial apparatus dimensions: effort wheel diameter \(D_{\text{effort}}\), load drum diameter \(D_{\text{drum}}\), worm starts \(n\), and wheel teeth \(T\).
  2. Calculate kinematic Velocity Ratio \(VR = \left(\frac{D_{\text{effort}}}{D_{\text{drum}}}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{T}{n}\right)\).
  3. Apply a calibrated weight \(W\) (e.g. \(10\text{ kgf}\)) to the load hanger.
  4. Incrementally add fractional weights to the effort pan until the effort wheel initiates steady, uniform rotation (or click Auto-Balance Effort).
  5. Record \(W\) and \(P\), and click Log Current Reading.
  6. Repeat across loads up to \(60\text{ kgf}\), or click Auto Sweep Standard Range.
  7. Review the regression card for empirical Law of the Machine constants (\(m\) and \(C\)).
3. Self-Locking vs. Overhauling Verification
Click Release / Snip Effort (Self-Locking Test) to simulate immediate loss of input tension:
  • In Single-Start Mode (\(n=1, \mu=0.12\)): \(\eta \approx 30\% - 42\% < 50\%\). The rig holds firmly in static equilibrium without dropping.
  • In Quad-Start Mode (\(n=4, \mu=0.12\)): \(\eta > 50\%\). Thread friction is insufficient to counteract backdrive torque, causing the load to drop rapidly in an overhauling runaway.
