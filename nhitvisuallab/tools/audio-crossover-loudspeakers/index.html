Audio Crossover & Loudspeakers Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Passive LC crossover dividing networks split multi-channel audio power into distinct frequency bands matched to specialized transducers (woofers for low frequencies, tweeters for high frequencies). A 2nd-order Butterworth design provides flat acoustic power summing ($L = \frac{R\sqrt{2}}{2\pi f_c}, C = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{2} f_c R}$) with $12\text{ dB/octave}$ attenuation roll-off.
