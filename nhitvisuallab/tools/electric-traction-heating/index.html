Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S5/UTHD Utilization, Traction, Heating & Drives

1. Mechanics of Train Movement

Tractive effort for acceleration $F_a = 277.8 \frac{W_e \alpha}{v}$, tractive effort for track gradient $F_g = 98.1 W G$, and train resistance $F_r = W \cdot r$. Total tractive effort $F_t = F_a \pm F_g + F_r\text{ (N)}$.

2. Specific Energy Consumption (SEC)

$\text{SEC} = \frac{\text{Total Energy Consumption in Watt-hours}}{\text{Train Weight in Tons} \times \text{Distance in km}}$. Reduced by coasting and regenerative braking returning power to $25\text{kV}$ OHE.

3. Electric Heating Methods

Dielectric Heating: $P = 2\pi f C V^2 \tan \delta\text{ (Watts)}$ at radio frequencies ($10-40\text{MHz}$) for uniform heating of non-conductors (plastics, plywood).
Induction Heating: Eddy currents and hysteresis for metal billet surface hardening.

4. Electric Arc & Resistance Welding

AC transformer vs DC inverter welding power sources. Seam, spot, and projection resistance welding heat generation $H = I^2 R t$.
