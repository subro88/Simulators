RCC One-Way & Two-Way Slab Design Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

RCC slabs are plate structural elements spanning across supporting walls or beams. When $L_y / L_x \le 2$, two-way bending action distributes moments across both orthogonal directions ($M_{ux} = \alpha_x w l_x^2, M_{uy} = \alpha_y w l_x^2$). Corner torsional steel ($0.75 A_{st,max}$) prevents uplifting at discontinuous exterior edges.
