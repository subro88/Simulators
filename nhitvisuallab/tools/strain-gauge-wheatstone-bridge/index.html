Strain Gauge & Wheatstone Bridge Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Bonded metallic foil strain gauges convert mechanical surface deformation into fractional electrical resistance changes via the piezoresistive Gauge Factor ($GF = \frac{\Delta R/R}{\epsilon}$). Wheatstone bridge configurations translate minute resistance shifts ($\Delta R \approx 0.1\ \Omega$) into proportional millivolt outputs ($V_o = \frac{V_s}{4}\frac{\Delta R}{R}$ for quarter-bridge).
