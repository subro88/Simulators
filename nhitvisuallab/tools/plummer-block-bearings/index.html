Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE ME/S3/MED Machine Bearings & Plummer Block Lab

1. Hydrodynamic Lubrication (Reynold's Theory)

Wedge action drags viscous oil between converging journal and bearing surfaces, building fluid pressure that completely separates metal surfaces.

2. Sommerfeld Number

$S = \left(\frac{r}{c}\right)^2 \frac{\mu n_s}{P}$, dimensionless bearing characteristic parameter relating clearance, viscosity, speed, and unit load.

3. Heat Generation & Dissipation

$H_g = \mu W v$ (Watts). Heat dissipated by bearing surface $H_d = C \cdot A \cdot (T_b - T_a)$. In thermal equilibrium $H_g = H_d$.

4. Plummer Block Construction

Split brasses lined with Babbitt metal (tin/lead base white metal) to prevent shaft scoring during starting/stopping.

Plummer blocks (pedestal bearings) support rotating transmission shafts subjected to heavy radial loads. Proper Sommerfeld rating guarantees non-contact thick film hydrodynamic lubrication.
