📖 Faraday's Laws of Electrolysis Theoretical Principles

1. Faraday’s Second Law of Electrolysis

When an identical quantity of electric charge ($Q$) is passed through different electrolytic solutions connected in series, the masses ($m$) of substances liberated or deposited at the electrodes are directly proportional to their chemical equivalent weights ($E$):

$$\frac{m_{\text{Ag}}}{E_{\text{Ag}}} = \frac{m_{\text{Cu}}}{E_{\text{Cu}}} = \frac{m_{\text{H}_2}}{E_{\text{H}}} = \frac{m_{\text{O}_2}}{E_{\text{O}}} = \frac{Q}{F}$$

2. Table of Chemical Equivalent Weights ($E = M/z$)

• Silver ($\text{Ag}$): $E_{\text{Ag}} = \frac{107.87}{1} = 107.87\text{ g/eq}, \quad Z_{\text{Ag}} = 1.1180 \times 10^{-3}\text{ g/C}$

• Copper ($\text{Cu}$): $E_{\text{Cu}} = \frac{63.55}{2} = 31.77\text{ g/eq}, \quad Z_{\text{Cu}} = 0.3293 \times 10^{-3}\text{ g/C}$

• Hydrogen ($\text{H}_2$): $E_{\text{H}} = \frac{2.016}{2} = 1.008\text{ g/eq}, \quad Z_{\text{H}} = 0.0104 \times 10^{-3}\text{ g/C}$

• Oxygen ($\text{O}_2$): $E_{\text{O}} = \frac{32.00}{4} = 8.000\text{ g/eq}, \quad Z_{\text{O}} = 0.0829 \times 10^{-3}\text{ g/C}$

3. Universal Faraday Constant ($F$)

$$F = N_A \cdot e = (6.02214 \times 10^{23}) \times (1.602176 \times 10^{-19}) \approx 96,485.3\text{ C/eq}$$

📖 Comprehensive User Manual & Experimental Procedure

1. Four-Phase Series Electrolysis Workflow

Phase 1: Pre-weigh dry Silver Cathode ($m_{1,\text{Ag}}$) and Copper Cathode ($m_{1,\text{Cu}}$) on the 4-decimal analytical balance dock. Bleed air from Hofmann limbs ($V_0 = 0.0\text{ mL}$).

Phase 2: Set circuit current $I$ (e.g., $0.800\text{ A}$) and run time $t$ ($30\text{ mins} = 1800\text{ s}$). Observe simultaneous $\text{Ag}$ dendrite growth, $\text{Cu}$ deposition, and $\text{H}_2/\text{O}_2$ gas displacement.

Phase 3: Wash, dry, and re-weigh Silver Cathode ($m_{2,\text{Ag}}$) and Copper Cathode ($m_{2,\text{Cu}}$). Read gas volumes $V_{\text{H}_2}$ and $V_{\text{O}_2}$.

Phase 4: Verify chemical equivalent ratios $\frac{m_{\text{Ag}}}{m_{\text{Cu}}} \approx 3.395$ and $\frac{V_{\text{H}_2}}{V_{\text{O}_2}} = 2:1$. Calculate Faraday constant $F = \frac{Q}{m/E}$.

2. Gas STP Volume to Mass Reduction

$$V_{\text{STP}} = V_{\text{observed}} \times \left(\frac{P_{\text{atm}} - P_{\text{aqueous}}}{101.325\text{ kPa}}\right) \times \left(\frac{273.15\text{ K}}{T_{\text{ambient}}}\right)$$

$$m_{\text{H}_2} = \frac{V_{\text{STP}}}{22414\text{ mL/mol}} \times 2.0158\text{ g/mol}$$
