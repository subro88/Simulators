📖 Electrochemistry & Metrology Theoretical Principles

1. Daniell Cell & Nernst Equation

The Daniell cell is a galvanic electrochemical cell consisting of a zinc anode in $\text{ZnSO}_4(aq)$ and a copper cathode in $\text{CuSO}_4(aq)$ connected by a $\text{KNO}_3 / \text{KCl}$ salt bridge:

$$\text{Anode (Oxidation): } \text{Zn}(s) \longrightarrow \text{Zn}^{2+}(aq) + 2e^- \quad (E^\circ = -0.763\text{ V})$$ $$\text{Cathode (Reduction): } \text{Cu}^{2+}(aq) + 2e^- \longrightarrow \text{Cu}(s) \quad (E^\circ = +0.337\text{ V})$$ $$E_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{R \cdot T}{n \cdot F} \ln\left(\frac{[\text{Zn}^{2+}]}{[\text{Cu}^{2+}]}\right) = 1.100 - \frac{0.05916}{2} \log_{10}\left(\frac{[\text{Zn}^{2+}]}{[\text{Cu}^{2+}]}\right)$$

2. Gibbs Free Energy ($\Delta G$) & Cell Work

$$\Delta G = -n \cdot F \cdot E_{\text{cell}} \quad (F = 96485.3\text{ C/mol})$$

3. 10-Meter Slide-Wire Potentiometer

A potentiometer measures EMF without drawing current at null balance ($I_G = 0$):

$$E_x = E_s \cdot \frac{l_x}{l_s}, \qquad r = R \cdot \left(\frac{l_1 - l_2}{l_2}\right)$$

4. Carey Foster 4-Gap Resistance Bridge

Compares two small nearly equal resistances $X$ and $Y$ independently of end resistance errors ($\alpha, \beta$) and ratio coils ($P, Q$):

$$\mathbf{X - Y = \rho \cdot (l_2 - l_1)} \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad \mathbf{X_{\text{unknown}} = R + \rho \cdot (l_1' - l_2')}$$

📖 Comprehensive User Manual & Experimental Procedure

1. Daniell Cell Nernst Equation & Thermodynamics

The Daniell cell converts chemical energy into electrical energy. At standard state ($1.0\text{ M}$, $298.15\text{ K}$), $E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = +0.337 - (-0.763) = 1.100\text{ V}$. Varying $[\text{Zn}^{2+}]$ or $[\text{Cu}^{2+}]$ alters cell potential according to the Nernst equation: $E = E^\circ - \frac{0.05916}{2} \log_{10}([\text{Zn}^{2+}]/[\text{Cu}^{2+}])$.

2. 10-Meter Slide-Wire Potentiometer Procedure

Plug driver circuit key, slide jockey along the 10 parallel wire segments until galvanometer needle points to zero ($I_G = 0$). Note balancing length $l_s$ for standard cell and $l_x$ for unknown cell. Unknown EMF is $E_x = E_s \cdot (l_x / l_s)$.

3. Carey Foster 4-Gap Bridge Procedure

Stage 1 (Calibrate $\rho$): Insert $0.100\ \Omega$ standard in Gap 1 and copper strip ($0\ \Omega$) in Gap 4. Find balance $l_1$. Swap outer gaps (copper strip in Gap 1, $0.100\ \Omega$ in Gap 4) and find $l_2$. Wire resistivity is $\rho = \frac{0.100}{|l_1 - l_2|}$.

Stage 2 (Measure $X_{\text{unknown}}$): Insert unknown specimen in Gap 1 and known fractional resistor $R$ ($0.050\ \Omega$) in Gap 4. Find $l_1'$ and $l_2'$. Compute $X_{\text{unknown}} = R + \rho \cdot (l_1' - l_2')$.

Stage 3 (Eliminate Thermoelectric Error): Toggle Commutator key to reverse battery polarity and average balance readings.
