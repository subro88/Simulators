LVDT Linear Displacement Transducer Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

The Linear Variable Differential Transformer (LVDT) measures linear mechanical displacement without electrical contact. Symmetrically positioned, series-opposing secondary coils ($S_1, S_2$) induce differential AC voltage ($V_{out} = V_{S1} - V_{S2}$) directly proportional to core displacement from magnetic null, reversing phase by $180^\circ$ across null.
