1. Vicat Standard Consistency

Defined as the water percentage required to permit a Vicat plunger of 10 mm diameter to penetrate to a point 5 to 7 mm from the bottom of the Vicat mould (IS 4031 Part 4).

2. Setting Time Criteria

Initial setting time must not be less than 30 minutes for OPC, and final setting time must not exceed 600 minutes (10 hours).

Guided Experiment: Standard Consistency Determination

1. Adjust water percentage slider $P\%$ until Vicat plunger penetration reaches 5-7 mm from the bottom of the mould.
2. Record the exact $P\%$ value for setting time tests ($0.85 P$).
