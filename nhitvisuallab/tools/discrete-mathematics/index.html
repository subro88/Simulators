Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE CST/3/301 Discrete Mathematics

1. Mathematical Logic & Inference

Propositional calculus, truth tables, tautologies, contradictions, and duality laws. Conditional ($P \to Q \equiv \neg P \lor Q$) and Biconditional ($P \leftrightarrow Q \equiv (P \to Q) \land (Q \to P)$) equivalence.

2. Set Theory & Operations

Union ($A \cup B$), Intersection ($A \cap B$), Difference ($A - B$), and Cartesian product ($A \times B$). Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion: $|A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B|$.

3. Binary Relations & Partitions

A relation $R$ on set $A$ is an Equivalence Relation if it satisfies Reflexivity ($\forall a, aRa$), Symmetry ($aRb \implies bRa$), and Transitivity ($aRb \land bRc \implies aRc$).

4. Graph Theory & Handshaking Lemma

Handshaking Theorem: $\sum_{v \in V} \text{deg}(v) = 2|E|$. An undirected graph has an even number of vertices of odd degree. Eulerian circuit exists if all vertex degrees are even.
