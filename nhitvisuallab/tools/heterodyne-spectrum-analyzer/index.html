Heterodyne Spectrum Analyzer Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Spectrum Analyzers perform Fourier domain amplitude-versus-frequency decomposition of complex signals using a swept superheterodyne receiver. Narrowing Resolution Bandwidth (RBW) lowers Displayed Average Noise Floor ($DANL = -174\text{ dBm/Hz} + 10\log_{10}(RBW) + NF$) and resolves close-in spurious emissions and harmonic distortion ($THD$).

1. Overview

Spectrum Analyzers perform Fourier domain amplitude-versus-frequency decomposition of complex signals using a swept superheterodyne receiver. Narrowing Resolution Bandwidth (RBW) lowers Displayed Average Noise Floor ($DANL = -174\text{ dBm/Hz} + 10\log_{10}(RBW) + NF$) and resolves close-in spurious emissions and harmonic distortion ($THD$).
