Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE ME/S3/MP1 Welding Technology & HAZ Virtual Lab

1. Net Heat Input Equation

$H_{net} = \frac{\eta \cdot V \cdot I \cdot 60}{v \cdot 1000} \text{ (kJ/mm)}$. Higher heat input broadens the HAZ and coarsens grain structure.

2. Heat Affected Zone (HAZ)

Unmelted base metal adjacent to fusion line experiencing high thermal cycle: Grain growth zone, Recrystallization zone, and Partial transformation zone.

3. Weldability & Carbon Equivalent

$CE = \%C + \frac{\%Mn}{6} + \frac{\%Cr + \%Mo + \%V}{5} + \frac{\%Ni + \%Cu}{15}$. If $CE > 0.40\%$, preheating is required.

4. Gas Welding & Flames

Neutral flame ($1:1\text{ O}_2\text{:C}_2\text{H}_2, 3200^\circ\text{C}$), Oxidizing flame (excess $\text{O}_2$), and Carburizing reducing flame.
