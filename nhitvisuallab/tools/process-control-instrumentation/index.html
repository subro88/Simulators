Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S6/PC Process Control & Instrumentation

1. 4-20mA Current Loop Standard

Two-wire transmitter standard where $4\text{mA}$ represents live zero ($0\%$) and $20\text{mA}$ represents full scale span ($100\%$). Immune to lead wire voltage drops and cable resistance over long plant distances.

2. Control Valve Flow Coefficient ($C_v$)

$C_v = Q \sqrt{\frac{SG}{\Delta P}}$, defined as flow of water in US GPM at $60^\circ\text{F}$ that passes through the valve with a $1.0\text{ psi}$ pressure drop.

3. Pneumatic Diaphragm Actuators

Air-to-Open (Fail-Close, FC) vs Air-to-Close (Fail-Open, FO) safety mechanisms controlled via $3-15\text{ psig}$ pneumatic signal converted by I/P current-to-pressure converters.

4. Temperature & Flow Transducers

RTD Pt100 ($R_t = R_0(1 + \alpha t)$), Thermocouple Seebeck voltage, and Electromagnetic / Ultrasonic flowmeters.
