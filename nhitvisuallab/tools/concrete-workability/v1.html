1. Slump Cone Test (IS 1199)

A hollow frustum mould (top dia 100mm, bottom dia 200mm, height 300mm) is filled with concrete in 4 equal layers, each tamped 25 times with a 16mm bullet-nosed rod. The mould is lifted vertically, and the subsidence is measured as the slump in millimeters.

2. Compacting Factor Test

Measures the workability of low and very low workability concrete through standardized free fall from an upper hopper to a lower hopper, then into a receiving cylinder: CF = (Weight of Partially Compacted Concrete) / (Weight of Fully Compacted Concrete).

Laboratory Procedure: Compacting Factor Calculation

1. Clean and weigh the empty cylinder ($W_1$).
2. Place concrete in the upper hopper and open the trap door to let it fall into the lower hopper.
3. Release the lower trap door to let the concrete fall into the cylinder.
4. Weigh the partially compacted cylinder ($W_2$).
5. Refill the cylinder in layers and fully compact on a vibrating table, weighing fully compacted cylinder ($W_3$).
6. Compute: $$CF = \frac{W_2 - W_1}{W_3 - W_1}$$
