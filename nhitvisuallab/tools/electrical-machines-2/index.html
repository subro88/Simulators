Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S4/EM II Electrical Machines - II

1. 3-Phase Induction Motor Torque-Slip

Developed torque $T = \frac{3}{\omega_s} \frac{s E_2^2 R_2}{R_2^2 + (s X_2)^2}$. Max torque $T_{max} = \frac{3}{2\omega_s} \frac{E_2^2}{2 X_2}$ is independent of rotor resistance $R_2$, but slip at max torque $s_{max} = \frac{R_2}{X_2}$ increases with $R_2$.

2. Synchronous Alternator & V-Curves

Excitation characteristic curves (Armature current $I_a$ vs Field current $I_f$). Under-excitation draws lagging reactive power; over-excitation delivers leading reactive power for power factor correction (Synchronous Condenser).

3. Single Phase Induction Motors

Double revolving field theory (Forward field $s$ and Backward field $2-s$). Starting methods: Split-phase, Capacitor-start, Capacitor-run, and Shaded-pole motors.

4. Speed Control & Starters

Star-Delta ($1/\sqrt{3}$ voltage reduction), Direct-On-Line (DOL), Auto-transformer starters, and V/f scalar inverter drive speed regulation.
