Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S5/PED Power Electronics & Drives

1. SCR Phase-Controlled Rectifiers

Single-phase fully controlled bridge converter average DC voltage: $V_{dc} = \frac{2 V_m}{\pi} \cos \alpha$. For $\alpha < 90^\circ$, it operates as a rectifier (power flow AC $\to$ DC); for $\alpha > 90^\circ$ with inductive R-L load, it operates as a line-commutated inverter (DC $\to$ AC).

2. DC-DC Chopper Drives

Buck Converter: $V_o = D V_s$ ($D < 1$, step-down).
Boost Converter: $V_o = \frac{V_s}{1 - D}$ ($D < 1$, step-up). Used in variable DC traction motor speed control.

3. Sinusoidal PWM Inverters

Comparing high-frequency triangular carrier with reference sine wave generates variable-frequency, variable-voltage (V/f) AC drives for 3-phase induction motors.

4. Power Semiconductor Devices

SCR, TRIAC, GTO, Power MOSFET (high frequency), and IGBT (high voltage & current with low conduction losses).
