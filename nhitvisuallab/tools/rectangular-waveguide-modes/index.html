Rectangular Waveguide Modes Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Rectangular hollow metal waveguides guide microwave electromagnetic waves above the cutoff frequency $f_c = \frac{c}{2a}$ with zero radiation loss. In the dominant $TE_{10}$ mode, guide wavelength exceeds free-space wavelength ($\lambda_g = \frac{\lambda_0}{\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}}$), phase velocity exceeds the speed of light ($v_p > c$), and group velocity carries signal power ($v_p v_g = c^2$).
