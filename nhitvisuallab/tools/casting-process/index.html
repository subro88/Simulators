Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE ME/S3/MP1 Casting Processes

1. Pattern Allowances

Shrinkage Allowance: Compensates for volumetric contraction during cooling ($10-20\text{ mm/m}$).
Draft Allowance: Taper angle ($1^\circ-3^\circ$) for easy withdrawal without damaging mold cavity.
Machining & Distortion: Extra $2-5\text{ mm}$ for surface finish.

2. Chvorinov's Rule for Solidification

Total solidification time $t_s = B \left(\frac{V}{A}\right)^2$, where $V$ is casting volume, $A$ is cooling surface area, and $B$ is the mold thermal constant. Riser modulus must exceed casting modulus by at least $20\%$ ($M_r \ge 1.2 M_c$).

3. Gating System & Choke Area

Choke Area $A_c = \frac{M}{\rho \cdot t_p \cdot C_d \sqrt{2 g H}}$ regulates molten metal flow rate. Pressurized gating ($1:2:1$) vs Unpressurized gating ($1:2:2$) avoids aspiration of atmospheric gases.

4. Casting Defects & Cupola Operation

Diagnosis of blowholes, pinholes, cold shuts, misruns, and hot tears. Cupola charge ratio ($8:1$ Iron-to-Coke) and flux addition ($40\text{ kg}$ limestone per ton).

📖 User Guide — Casting Process & Pattern Sizing Virtual Lab
1 Overview

The Casting Process Virtual Lab simulates sand casting mold preparation, pattern dimensioning with thermal shrinkage allowances, gating sprue velocity, and Chvorinov's solidification cooling dynamics in compliance with WBSCTE Manufacturing Process - I (ME/S3/MP1).

2 Setting Up the Load Case

Select your casting alloy from the dropdown (Grey Cast Iron, Aluminium, Cast Steel, Brass). Enter the desired casting nominal dimensions, part volume $V$, and cooling surface area $A$. The simulator automatically calculates the oversized pattern dimensions required.

3 Building the Diagrams

The left canvas visualizes the mold cross-section with Cope (top) and Drag (bottom) flasks, gating sprue, runner, and casting cavity. Click 'Simulate Molten Pour' to animate liquid metal filling the mold at Torricelli velocity.

4 Reading the Theory

Switch to the 'Casting Theory' tab to review pattern allowances (shrinkage, draft, shake), gating choke equations, unpressurized gating ratios, and common foundry casting defects.

5 Try a Problem

Practice calculating the solidification time for a cube vs sphere of equal volume, and verify that the sphere solidifies slowest due to its minimal surface area-to-volume ratio.

6 Design Notes
  • Cast steel requires the highest shrinkage allowance ($20\text{ mm/m}$ or $2.0\%$).
  • Ensure the riser modulus is at least $1.2\times$ the casting modulus so directional solidification prevents shrinkage cavities in the main part.

Foundry Technology: Sand Casting, Pattern Allowances & Solidification Mechanics

Metal casting is one of the oldest and most versatile manufacturing processes where molten metal is poured into a refractory mold cavity containing the negative shape of the desired component. Upon cooling and solidification, the metal part is broken out of the mold and fettled.
