Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE ME/S3/MP1 Metal Forming & Forging Virtual Lab

1. Flat Rolling Mechanics

Draft $\Delta h = h_0 - h_1$. Maximum possible draft without slipping $\Delta h_{max} = \mu^2 R$. Contact arc length $L = \sqrt{R \Delta h}$.

2. Upset Forging & Friction Hill

Cylindrical billet compressed between flat platens. Friction hill pressure distribution $p(r) = \sigma_0 e^{\frac{2\mu}{h}(R-r)}$.

3. Wire Drawing Stress

Drawing stress $\sigma_d = \sigma_0 \ln(A_0 / A_1) (1 + \mu / \alpha)$. Maximum theoretical single-pass reduction is $63.2\%$ without wire breakage.

4. Extrusion & Forming Defects

Direct vs Indirect extrusion. Extrusion ratio $R = A_0 / A_1$. Center-burst chevron cracking, piping, and surface tearing prevention.

Metal Forming Mechanics: Rolling Force, Upset Forging & Wire Drawing

Metal forming involves shaping solid metals through plastic deformation above or below recrystallization temperature without removing chips. Understanding roll force, friction hills, and drawing stress ensures energy-efficient tooling design.
