Cellular Frequency Reuse & SIR Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Cellular networks partition service coverage areas into hexagonal cells, reusing allocated frequencies across clusters of size $K = i^2 + ij + j^2$ ($K=7$). The co-channel reuse ratio ($Q = D/R = \sqrt{3K}$) guarantees adequate Signal-to-Interference Ratio ($S/I = \frac{(\sqrt{3K})^\gamma}{6} \ge 18\text{ dB}$) against the 6 first-tier interfering base stations.
