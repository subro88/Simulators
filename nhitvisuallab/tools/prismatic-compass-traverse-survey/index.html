Prismatic Compass Traverse Survey Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Prismatic compass surveying determines magnetic bearings of survey traverse lines relative to the Earth's magnetic meridian. The whole circle bearing (WCB, $0^\circ - 360^\circ$) is converted to reduced bearing (RB). Discrepancies between forward and back bearings ($|BB - FB| \ne 180^\circ$) indicate local magnetic attractions from buried steel or power lines, corrected via included angle computations.

1. Overview

Prismatic compass surveying determines magnetic bearings of survey traverse lines relative to the Earth's magnetic meridian. The whole circle bearing (WCB, $0^\circ - 360^\circ$) is converted to reduced bearing (RB). Discrepancies between forward and back bearings ($|BB - FB| \ne 180^\circ$) indicate local magnetic attractions from buried steel or power lines, corrected via included angle computations.

2. Setting Up the Load Case & Parameters

Use the control sliders on the left to configure survey readings, batch weights, or beam load configurations. Click Calculate & Analyze to run the solvers.

3. Building the Diagrams & Diagnostic Telemetry

The interactive canvas dynamically plots real-time survey profiles, SFD/BMD diagrams, or material stress distributions corresponding to the exact state equations.

4. Reading the Theory
