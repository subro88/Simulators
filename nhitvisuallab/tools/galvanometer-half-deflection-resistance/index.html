📖 Mathematical Theory & Formulas

BS104/BS106 Applied Physics-II: Galvanometer Internal Resistance G = RS/(R-S) & Figure of Merit k = E/((R+G)theta)

Calculate galvanometer internal resistance: $$G = \frac{R \cdot S}{R - S}$$

Understand the half-deflection method: Initially key $K_1$ closed with high $R$ gives deflection $\theta = \frac{E}{R+G}$.

Determine the figure of merit $k$: $$k = \frac{E}{(R + G)\theta}$$ and full scale deflection current $I_g = k \cdot \theta_{max}$.
