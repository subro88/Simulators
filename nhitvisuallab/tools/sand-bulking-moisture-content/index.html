Sand Bulking & Moisture Content Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Moisture films surrounding sand grains push adjacent particles apart via surface tension, causing a maximum volumetric expansion (bulking) of $20-30\%$ at $4-6\%$ moisture content. Inundating the sand with water breaks the surface meniscus, recovering true compacted volume ($Bulking\% = \frac{h_2 - h_1}{h_1} \times 100\%$).
