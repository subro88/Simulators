📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

Resistance Temperature Detectors (Pt100 RTDs) utilize the positive temperature coefficient of pure platinum governed by the Callendar-Van Dusen equation ($R_t = R_0(1 + AT + BT^2)$). Thermocouples generate thermoelectric EMF via the Seebeck effect ($E = S \Delta T$), providing rugged temperature sensing across industrial furnaces.
