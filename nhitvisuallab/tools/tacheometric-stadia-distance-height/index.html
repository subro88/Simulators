Tacheometric Stadia Distance & Height Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Tacheometric surveying determines both horizontal distances and elevation differences electronically or optically without chain or tape measurement. By intercepting vertical stadia rod readings ($s = \text{Upper} - \text{Lower}$), inclined sights evaluate distance ($D = k s \cos^2\theta + c\cos\theta$) and vertical height ($V = \frac{k s}{2}\sin 2\theta + c\sin\theta$) rapidly across broken topography.
