Superheterodyne Radio Receiver Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

The superheterodyne receiver overcomes the tracking and selectivity shortcomings of TRF radios by downconverting all incoming RF carriers $f_s$ to a fixed Intermediate Frequency ($IF = 455\text{ kHz}$ for AM, $10.7\text{ MHz}$ for FM) using a local oscillator and mixer. Image frequency interference occurs at $f_{img} = f_s + 2 IF$.
