Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S3/WS Electrical Workshop Practice

1. Staircase & Godown Wiring

Two-way SPDT switch wiring allows independent control of a single corridor/staircase lamp from two different locations (top and bottom of staircase).

2. Earthing Systems (Pipe & Plate)

Pipe electrode earthing uses perforated GI pipe surrounded by charcoal and salt layers. Indian Standard IS 3043 requires domestic earth resistance $R \le 5.0 \Omega$ and substation $R \le 1.0 \Omega$.

3. Megger Earth Tester

Hand-driven DC generator (500V/1000V) or electronic digital insulation tester measuring mega-ohm insulation resistance between phase conductors and earth.

4. MCB & ELCB/RCCB Protection

MCB: Bimetallic strip for thermal overload and electromagnetic solenoid for short-circuit trip.
RCCB: Detects residual leakage current $\ge 30\text{mA}$ to prevent fatal electrocution.
