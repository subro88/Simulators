01Multi-Way Switching Logic

Two SPDT two-way switches control one lamp from two positions. With strappers between and , the lamp is ON when both switches occupy the same throw:

  • Phase must pass through the switches; the neutral goes directly to the lamp holder — this is the IS 732 polarity requirement.
  • The Carter method puts phase and neutral on the outer terminals and leaves the holder live when OFF: IE Rule violation.
  • An intermediate (DPDT) switch extends two-way control to three or more positions.

Standard operating procedure

  1. Select a workshop module, then use the wire spools to connect brass terminals on the wiring board; press the main DP only after the safety audit passes.
  2. For insulation tests, isolate the mains, select the lead pair, hold the 500 V crank and read the Megger in mega-ohms.
  3. For earth testing, drag the potential spike along the tape, log seven readings, and pick the 62% plateau value.
  4. For MCB studies, choose a curve and rating, switch workshop loads, and watch the thermal/magnetic trip on the log-log curve.
  5. Log readings into the practical record, export the report, and attempt the nine assessed tasks and trade quiz.
