Flow Net & Seepage Exit Gradient Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

2D flow nets solve the Laplace seepage equation ($\nabla^2 \phi = 0$) beneath dams and weirs. Seepage discharge per unit width is $q = k H \frac{N_f}{N_d}$. The downstream exit gradient ($i_{exit} = \frac{\Delta h}{l}$) must remain below $1/5$ to $1/6$ to prevent boiling, soil quicksand conditions, and destructive foundation piping.
