Soil Consolidation & Oedometer Settlement Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Soil consolidation is the gradual reduction in saturated clay volume due to expulsion of pore water under sustained structural loads. Terzaghi's One-Dimensional Consolidation equation calculates ultimate primary consolidation settlement ($S_c = \frac{C_c H_0}{1+e_0}\log_{10}\frac{\sigma_0' + \Delta\sigma'}{\sigma_0'}$) from oedometer $e-\log\sigma'$ compressibility curves.

📖 User Guide & Operating Manual

1. Overview

Soil consolidation is the gradual reduction in saturated clay volume due to expulsion of pore water under sustained structural loads. Terzaghi's One-Dimensional Consolidation equation calculates ultimate primary consolidation settlement ($S_c = \frac{C_c H_0}{1+e_0}\log_{10}\frac{\sigma_0' + \Delta\sigma'}{\sigma_0'}$) from oedometer $e-\log\sigma'$ compressibility curves.
