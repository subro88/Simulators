Syllabus Coverage — WBSCTE EE/S5/SGP Switchgear & Protection

1. IDMT Overcurrent Relay Characteristics

Standard Inverse (IEC 60255): $t = \frac{0.14}{(PSM)^{0.02} - 1} \times TMS$.
Plug Setting Multiplier $PSM = \frac{I_{fault}}{\text{Plug Setting} \times CT_{sec}}$. Time Multiplier Setting ($TMS$) shifts curve vertically for graded discrimination.

2. Differential Protection (Merz-Price)

Operates on vector difference of current entering and leaving the protected zone ($|I_1 - I_2| > k \frac{I_1 + I_2}{2}$). Biased restraining coils prevent false tripping under CT saturation during external through-faults.

3. Circuit Breakers & Arc Extinction

Restriking Voltage & Rate of Rise of Restriking Voltage ($RRRV = \omega_n V_m \sin \omega_n t$). Vacuum Circuit Breakers (VCBs) utilize rapid dielectric recovery of metallic vapor in ultra-high vacuum ($10^{-7}\text{bar}$).

4. Neutral Earthing & Surge Arresters

Solid vs Resistance Earthing to limit Earth Fault current. Metal Oxide Surge Arresters (ZnO) protect power transformers from atmospheric lightning and switching overvoltage surges.
