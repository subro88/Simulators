RCC Isolated Footing & Punching Shear Lab — Theory & Governing Equations

Isolated column footings spread heavy concentrated axial column loads over a large soil area so that safe bearing capacity (SBC) is not exceeded. Critical design checks include: 1) One-way beam shear at distance $d$ from the column face, and 2) Two-way punching shear along perimeter $b_0 = 4(a+d)$ at distance $d/2$ ($\tau_{vp} \le 0.25\sqrt{f_{ck}}$).
