1. Who We Are

NHIT VisualLab (NHIT VisualLab) is a free, browser-based platform providing interactive mechanical engineering simulators for technical and vocational education. It is operated independently by the NHIT VisualLab team.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit NHIT VisualLab, how it is used, and what choices you have.

2. What Data Is Collected

NHIT VisualLab does not require you to create an account, submit personal information, or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information directly.

The following non-personal data is collected automatically through third-party services (Google Analytics and Google AdSense) when you visit the site:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Country and approximate region (derived from IP address, which Google anonymises)
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website
  • Device type (desktop, tablet, mobile)

This data is collected in aggregate and is used solely to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve the simulators and content.

3. Cookies We Use

NHIT VisualLab itself does not set first-party cookies except for cookie-notice dismissal (stored in localStorage, not a cookie). However, the third-party services embedded in this site do set cookies. These are:

  • Google Analytics cookies — to track aggregate site usage (see Section 4)
  • Google AdSense cookies — to show contextually relevant advertisements (see Section 5)

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Please note that blocking cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Third-Party Links

NHIT VisualLab may contain links to external websites (for example, educational references or manufacturer documentation). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

7. Your Rights & Opt-Out

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data-protection laws (including the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR, and similar regulations) including:

  • The right to know what data is held about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of data processing for advertising purposes

Because NHIT VisualLab does not collect personal data directly, most of these rights apply to the third-party services (Google) rather than to us. To exercise your rights with Google, visit Google’s Data & Privacy page.

To opt out of personalised advertising specifically:

  • Visit Google Ad Settings
  • Use the NAI opt-out tool
  • Enable “Do Not Track” in your browser settings

8. Children’s Privacy

NHIT VisualLab is designed for use by students in technical and vocational education programmes, including those who may be minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of NHIT VisualLab after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us via the Contact page.
