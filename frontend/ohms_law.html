1. Fundamental Ohm's Law

Current $I$ flowing through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the potential difference $V$ and inversely proportional to resistance $R$: $I = V / R$.

2. Electric Power & Joule Heating

Electrical energy converted into heat per second: $P = V I = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$ (Joules/sec or Watts).

Guided Experiment: Series vs Parallel Resistance

1. Set $R1 = 100\ \Omega$ and $R2 = 200\ \Omega$ in Series ($Req = 300\ \Omega$).
2. Switch topology to Parallel ($Req = 66.7\ \Omega$).
3. Observe total circuit current increases by 4.5x in parallel!
