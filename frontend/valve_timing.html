1. Valve Overlap

The brief period near Top Dead Center (TDC) when both intake and exhaust valves remain open simultaneously to utilize exhaust gas momentum for fresh charge scavenging.

2. Volumetric Efficiency

Higher valve lift and longer durations increase volumetric efficiency at high RPMs but reduce low-end idle stability.
