1. Ackermann Steering Principle

Ensures all wheels roll around a single common instantaneous center of rotation (ICR) during cornering to eliminate lateral tire scrubbing.

2. Scrub Radius

The distance at ground level between the kingpin steering axis projection and the center of the tire contact patch.
