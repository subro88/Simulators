1. Crankcase Compression

In cross-scavenged 2-stroke engines, the underside of the piston compresses the fresh air-fuel mixture inside the crankcase before transfer.

2. Transfer & Exhaust Ports

Instead of poppet valves, piston skirt displacement uncovers ports cut directly into the cylinder liner to transfer fresh charge and expel exhaust gases.

Guided Experiment: Power Density Comparison

Question: Why does a 2-stroke engine produce 1 power stroke per revolution?

  • ✓ Because intake/scavenging and exhaust processes occur simultaneously near Bottom Dead Center (BDC).
