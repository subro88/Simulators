1. Cycloidal Motion

Provides continuous velocity, acceleration, and zero jerk at terminal stroke ends, making it ideal for high-speed precision machinery.

2. Pressure Angle (α)

The angle between the direction of follower motion and the normal to the pitch curve. Should not exceed 30° to prevent follower binding in guide bushes.

Guided Experiment: Pressure Angle Reduction

1. Increase cam base circle radius $r_0$ from 30 mm to 80 mm.
2. Observe how larger base circles reduce the maximum pressure angle $\alpha$, preventing mechanical jamming!
