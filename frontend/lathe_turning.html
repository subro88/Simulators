Lathe Turning Physics & Merchant Circle Theory

Cutting Speed vc: vc = (π • D0 • N) / 1000 (m/min)

Material Removal Rate MRR: MRR = vc • f • ap (cm³/min)

Cutting Force Fc: Fc = kc • f • ap where kc is specific cutting pressure (N/mm²).
