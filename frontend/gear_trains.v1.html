1. Purpose of Idler Gears

Idler gears do not affect the overall speed ratio ($i = Z_{\text{out}} / Z_{\text{in}}$), but they change the direction of output rotation and bridge physical center distance gaps.

2. Compound Gear Trains

Mounting multiple gears on the same shaft enables massive speed reduction ratios in compact gearbox volumes.
