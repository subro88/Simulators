1. Inversion of Slider-Crank Mechanism

By fixing different links (crank, connecting rod, or slider), we get key industrial inversions: Whitworth quick return, Rotary IC engine, oscillating cylinder, and hand pump.

2. Obliquity Ratio Impact

A smaller obliquity ratio ($\lambda = r/l$) reduces side-thrust forces on the cylinder wall, reducing friction wear.

Guided Experiment: Peak Piston Velocity Angle

1. Set $r = 50\text{ mm}$ and $l = 200\text{ mm}$ ($\lambda = 0.25$).
2. Sweep crank angle $\theta$ from $0^\circ$ to $90^\circ$.
3. Observe peak velocity occurs slightly before $90^\circ$ (around $\theta \approx 76^\circ$) due to connecting rod obliquity!
