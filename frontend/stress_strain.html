1. Hooke's Law & Elasticity

Within the elastic limit, normal stress ($\sigma$) is directly proportional to normal strain ($\varepsilon$), governed by Young's Modulus ($E = \sigma / \varepsilon$).

2. Interrelation of Elastic Constants

For isotropic materials: $G = \frac{E}{2(1+\nu)}$ and $K = \frac{E}{3(1-2\nu)}$. Knowledge of any two constants defines the remaining two!

Guided Experiment: Elongation vs Diameter Relation

3. Observe cross-sectional area quadruples, dropping stress and elongation by 4x!
