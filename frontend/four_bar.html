1. Grashof's Criterion

States that for a 4-bar linkage, if $s + l \le p + q$, at least one link can make a complete 360° revolution relative to the ground frame.

2. Transmission Angle (μ)

The acute angle between the coupler link (c) and output rocker (d). For optimal force transmission without binding, $\mu \ge 40^\circ$.

Guided Experiment: Transmission Angle Optimization

1. Vary coupler link length $c$ and observe transmission angle $\mu$.
2. Notice if $\mu < 40^\circ$, mechanical efficiency drops and joint friction binding increases!
