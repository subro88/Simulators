1. V-Belt Wedging Action

V-belts utilize groove side friction ($\mu / \sin\beta$), increasing effective friction torque and allowing much higher power transmission than flat belts.

2. Centrifugal Tension ($T_c$)

At high speeds ($v > 20\text{ m/s}$), centrifugal force $T_c = m v^2$ reduces the effective contact pressure between belt and pulley.

Guided Experiment: Flat vs V-Belt Power Comparison

1. Keep all dimensions constant ($D_1=200\text{ mm}, D_2=400\text{ mm}, T_{\text{max}}=1500\text{ N}$).
2. Switch from Flat Belt to V-Belt.
3. Observe how V-belt wedging action significantly reduces slack side tension $T_2$ and increases transmitted power!
