1. Bernoulli's Conservation Principle

In an incompressible, inviscid fluid stream, total mechanical energy along a streamline remains constant: $\frac{P}{\rho g} + \frac{v^2}{2g} + z = C$. As fluid speeds up in the constriction, static pressure drops!

2. Venturimeter Discharge Measurement

The volumetric flow rate $Q = C_d \frac{A_1 A_2}{\sqrt{A_1^2 - A_2^2}} \sqrt{2g \Delta h}$ directly relates the differential manometer head $\Delta h$ to mass flow.

Guided Experiment: Throat Constriction & Velocity Multiplication

1. Set $d1 = 100\text{ mm}$ and $d2 = 50\text{ mm}$ (Area Ratio $A1/A2 = 4$).
2. Observe throat velocity $v2$ is exactly 4 times inlet velocity $v1$.
3. Notice static pressure drops significantly at the throat to conserve energy!
