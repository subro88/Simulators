1. Cylinder Block & Piston

The piston reciprocates inside the cylinder bore, converting high-pressure gas expansion from combustion into linear kinetic energy.

2. Connecting Rod & Crankshaft

Converts linear piston reciprocation into continuous rotary motion at the output flywheel via slider-crank kinematics.

3. Intake & Exhaust Valves

Camshaft-driven poppet valves that open and close at precise crank angles to control air/fuel induction and burnt gas scavenging.

4. Spark Plug / Fuel Injector

Provides electric spark ignition for Petrol (Otto) engines or high-pressure diesel atomization for Compression Ignition (C.I.) engines.

Thermodynamic Cycle Mathematical Formulation

1. Compression Ratio ($r$):

\[ r = \dfrac{V_{BDC}}{V_{TDC}} = \dfrac{V_s + V_c}{V_c} = 10.50 \]

2. Cylinder Swept Volume ($V_s$):

\( V_s = \dfrac{\pi}{4}B^2L = \dfrac{\pi}{4}(86.0\,\mathrm{mm})^2(86.0\,\mathrm{mm}) = 499.6\;\mathrm{cm^3} \)

3. Isentropic Compression Temperature ($T_2$):

\( T_2 = T_1 r^{\gamma-1} = 319.0 \times 10.50^{0.4} = \mathbf{817\;\mathrm{K}} \)

4. Peak Combustion Temperature ($T_3$):

\( T_3 = T_2 + \dfrac{q_{in}}{c_v} = 817 + \dfrac{1350}{0.718} = \mathbf{2699\;\mathrm{K}} \)

5. Ideal Otto Cycle Thermal Efficiency ($\eta_{th}$):

\[ \eta_{th} = 1 - \dfrac{1}{r^{\gamma-1}} = 1 - \dfrac{1}{10.50^{0.4}} = \mathbf{61.0\%} \]

6. Indicated Mean Effective Pressure ($\mathrm{IMEP}$):

\( \mathrm{IMEP} = \dfrac{W_{net}}{V_s} = \dfrac{0.5010\;\mathrm{kJ}}{499.6\;\mathrm{cm^3}} = \mathbf{10.03\;\mathrm{bar}} \)

7. Indicated Power Output ($\dot{W}_i$):

\[ \dot{W}_i = \mathrm{IMEP}\cdot V_s\cdot n_{cyl}\cdot \dfrac{N}{120} = \mathbf{41.8\;\mathrm{kW}} \]
